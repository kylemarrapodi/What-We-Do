Hudson HouseBeach Haven's No-Frills Neighborhood Dive Bar

Cold Beer, Cash Only, No Attitude

Tucked on a quiet side street on Long Beach Island, Hudson House — "The Hud" to regulars — is one of the oldest buildings on LBI and one of the last real dive bars left on the shore. Beer-sign walls, a shuffleboard table, darts, pool, a jukebox, and strong, affordable drinks. No food, no fuss, no credit cards.
