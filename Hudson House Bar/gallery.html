A Look Inside

A mix of real Hudson House photos and clearly-labeled stock photography standing in for the rest.

What We Pour
This is an independent design proposal, not photos taken on assignment for the bar. Only 2 of the 8 photos below are real — the exterior sign and the bar/drinks shot — both sourced directly from Hudson House's own public Yelp photo listing. Hudson House keeps a very low online profile (cash-only, no website), so the remaining 6 photos are free stock photography from Pexels and Wikimedia Commons, used only to illustrate the general vibe (shuffleboard, pool, darts, jukebox, a neon sign, a whiskey glass) — none of them depict the real bar, and every stock image is tagged below.
