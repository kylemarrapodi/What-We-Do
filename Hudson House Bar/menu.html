Call the Bar
💵  Cash only — no credit or debit cards accepted at Hudson House. There's no food served; it's strictly a beer-and-bar-games kind of place.
Hudson House doesn't publish a menu or price list anywhere online — it's a cash-only dive bar with a rotating, unpublicized lineup. Everything below is built from what's consistently described across reviews and the bar's own social media (research conducted August 2026): an extensive beer selection, affordable and strong drinks, and a fan-favorite Jack & Coke. No specific brands, taps, or prices are invented here — ask your bartender what's cold and pouring tonight.

🍺 Beer Selection

Reviewers consistently point to Hudson House's beer selection as one of its strengths — a solid rotating lineup of domestics, imports, and shore-bar staples. The exact tap and bottle list isn't published anywhere; it changes, and half the fun is finding out what's cold tonight.
