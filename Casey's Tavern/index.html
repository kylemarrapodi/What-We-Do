Cold Pints & Pub Grub on Woodside Street

A proper neighborhood Irish pub tucked on a quiet downtown Stamford street — cold pints, hearty comfort food, a pool table and dartboards in the back room, and an open patio for good-weather nights. Thursday open mic and Sunday karaoke keep regulars coming back.
