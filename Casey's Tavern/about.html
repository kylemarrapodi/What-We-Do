Our Story

A neighborhood tavern on Woodside Street, built around pints, pub food, and people who stick around.

View Menu

Casey's Tavern sits on a quiet side street just off the middle of downtown Stamford — the kind of place with a hand-painted sign out front, a pool table and dartboards in the back room, and regulars who know the bartender by name. It's a classic Irish-style neighborhood bar: unfussy, welcoming, and built for staying a while rather than rushing in and out.

The kitchen leans into hearty Irish-American pub food — shepherd's pie, chicken tipperary, corned beef reuben, and beer-battered fish and chips alongside an all-day Irish breakfast, wings, potato skins, and burgers. Behind the bar, Guinness, Harp, and Blue Moon pour on tap next to a well-stocked shelf of the usual bottles and a full backbar of liquor.

Cold pints, a warm welcome, and a bartender who remembers your order — Woodside Street's neighborhood pub.

What really sets the calendar is the weekly lineup: Thursday open mic night, which regulars consistently rate as one of the better open mics around, and karaoke every Sunday at 9 PM. Add in an open-air back patio for warm nights, TVs tuned to the game, and a pool table that's rarely empty, and it's easy to see why it's become a fixture for the downtown crowd.

Casey's keeps a low profile online — no flashy website, just word of mouth and a loyal base of regulars leaving four-star reviews on Yelp and Tripadvisor. This proposal imagines what a simple, modern site could look like if that changed.
