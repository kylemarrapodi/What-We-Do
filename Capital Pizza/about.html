Our Story

How a corner spot at 26th & Boston became the neighborhood pub of Tech Terrace.

View Menu

Capital Pizza opened its doors in January 2013 on the corner of 26th Street and Boston Avenue, becoming — in the restaurant's own words — the first and only restaurant/bar ever in the Tech Terrace neighborhood. The idea was simple: bring warmth and camaraderie to the block with hot, unique, satisfying pies and sandwiches, served in a space that felt like a proper neighborhood pub rather than a chain.

More than a decade later, Capital has grown into exactly that — a gathering spot where regulars know the bartenders, the walls are covered in Texas Tech pennants and Lubbock memorabilia, and the TVs are always tuned to whatever game matters that day. The menu centers on gourmet, made-to-order pizzas alongside calzones, wings, salads, and hearty sandwiches, with a gluten-free cauliflower crust option for guests who need it.

"Swing by for exceptional food & drink, meet the staff, and stay a while. We look forward to becoming not only your new favorite pizza stop in Lubbock, but also your friend."

The bar program is a point of real pride: Capital pours a rotating lineup of over 100 craft beers, bringing in new seasonal ales, porters, and stouts from breweries ranging from St. Arnold and Lagunitas to New Belgium and beyond, alongside a full selection of wines and liquors. Weekly specials keep the neighborhood coming back — $3 domestics and wells all day Monday and Saturday, and rotating Sunday specials from noon to 7 PM.

What started as a single corner bar has since grown to a second Lubbock location on Slide Road in the Lakeridge area, and the original Tech Terrace spot has picked up hardware along the way, including a Lubbock Avalanche-Journal Best Pizza award. Between the two locations, Capital Pizza has built a loyal following of 300+ reviews on Yelp and counting.
