Full Soles, Heel Repair & Leather Restoration Since Day One

Tucked into a storefront on Atlantic Street, Izet's has spent years giving worn-out shoes, boots, bags, and belts a second life — full sole and heel replacements, designer footwear restoration, and leather repair done the old-fashioned way, by hand. Reviewers keep coming back for the same reason: real craftsmanship, not a quick patch job.
