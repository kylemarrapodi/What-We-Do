Long Beach Island

Eighteen miles of barrier island off the central Jersey Shore — no chain restaurants, one road in, and a million reasons to come back every summer. LBI is live on The Concierge from Holgate at the southern tip to Barnegat Light in the north.

WildernessLive

Holgate

The wild, federally protected southern tip. Surf fishing, piping plovers, 3 miles of untouched beach, and beach driving in fall.
Main EventLive

Beach Haven

Fantasy Island, Thundering Surf, Surflight Theatre, Victorian history, 50+ restaurants, Chowderfest, and HopSauce. The heart of LBI.
GatewayLive

Ship Bottom

Where the causeway lands. Ship Bottom Brewery, The Arlington, Shellfish Co., and the island's best supply stops.
SurfingLive

Surf City

Mid-island hub. Surf City Hotel & Beach Club, Scojos, Saturday farmers market, bay beach, and the best biking on LBI.
Best DiningLive

Harvey Cedars

Black Eyed Susan's, Harvey Cedars Shellfish Co., Marigold Coffee, Birdy's. The best food town on the island — quietly exceptional.
IconicLive

Barnegat Light

Old Barney lighthouse (1859), Viking Village fishing fleet, Kubel's, Wally's, Daymark, and the widest beach on the Shore.

Getting to LBI

By Car: Route 72 East from the Garden State Parkway (Exit 63). One bridge, one road in. ~90 min from NYC, ~60 min from Philadelphia.

By Train: NJ Transit to Bay Head or Toms River, then cab/rideshare across the bay.

By Bike: Flat island-wide path. Rent in Ship Bottom or Surf City upon arrival.
