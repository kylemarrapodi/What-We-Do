Princeton

A 280-year-old college town in Mercer County, an hour from both New York and Philadelphia. Princeton runs two nightlife economies at once — an internationally programmed chamber series on the campus side of Nassau Street, and a handful of stubborn bars playing local bands until 2 AM on the other. Live on The Concierge across four neighborhoods.

Sea Bright

A three-mile ribbon of barrier peninsula in Monmouth County, wedged between the Atlantic Ocean and the Shrewsbury River — often called the first beach town at the Jersey Shore heading south from Sandy Hook. One dense strip of Ocean Avenue restaurants and membership beach clubs, rebuilt almost from scratch after Hurricane Sandy. Live on The Concierge as a single guide.

Montgomery Township

A 32-square-mile Somerset County township of old Dutch farmland and wealthy suburbs a few miles north of Princeton, built around three named villages — Skillman, Belle Mead and Blawenburg — rather than one downtown. Regularly ranked among New Jersey's wealthiest municipalities, with 247-acre Skillman Park, the historic Blawenburg crossroads, and the Route 206 corridor doing most of the everyday business. Cape May

The nation's oldest seaside resort, at the very southern tip of the state — and the only city in the country wholly designated a National Historic Landmark, for roughly 600 Victorian buildings rebuilt after an 1878 downtown fire. The pedestrian Washington Street Mall, the Emlen Physick Estate, Congress Hall and a nearly two-mile beachfront promenade all sit inside one compact, 2.5-square-mile city. Live on The Concierge as a single guide.

Getting to LBI

By Car: Route 72 East from the Garden State Parkway (Exit 63). One bridge, one road in. ~90 min from NYC, ~60 min from Philadelphia.

By Train: NJ Transit to Bay Head or Toms River, then cab/rideshare across the bay.

By Bike: Flat island-wide path. Rent in Ship Bottom or Surf City upon arrival.
