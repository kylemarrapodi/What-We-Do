Princeton, NJ

A 280-year-old college town that runs two nightlife economies at once — an internationally programmed concert series on one side of Nassau Street, and a handful of stubborn bars playing local bands until 2 AM on the other.

After Dark

Nightlife

Princeton's licensing rules do most of the sorting: three downtown bars can serve until 2 AM, and the residential-area bars have to close at midnight. Those three are where the town's nights actually happen.
