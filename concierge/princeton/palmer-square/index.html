Neighborhood

Palmer Square

The commercial heart of Princeton — a Colonial Revival square built between 1936 and 1939 by Edgar Palmer, anchored by the historic Nassau Inn and facing the University across Nassau Street. Shops and restaurants by day; by night, three of the only bars in town licensed to pour until 2 AM.

Where to Eat

Dining

Steakhouse

Witherspoon Grill

The square's dependable special-occasion room — steaks, a busy bar, and a patio that fills the moment the weather turns. Walking distance from everything on Palmer Square.

📍 Palmer Square · $$$ · Lunch & Dinner

French

Mistral

Small plates and a modern French-Mediterranean kitchen, and the more adventurous of the square's sit-down options. Same ownership lineage as Elements, Princeton's tasting-menu room.

📍 Palmer Square area · $$$ · Dinner

SeafoodLocal Favorite

Blue Point Grill

Open since 1999 and still the town's benchmark for fish — a raw bar and a daily catch list sourced from the seafood market next door. Indoor and patio seating, and a loyal following that books it out.

📍 258 Nassau St · $$$ · Dinner

Italian

La Mezzaluna

Long-running Italian on the square — pasta, a decent list, and tables outside facing the green. The kind of place that survives in a college town by being consistent rather than fashionable.

📍 Palmer Square · $$ · Lunch & Dinner

CoffeeInstitution

Small World Coffee

The town's coffee institution and the unofficial second office of half of Princeton. Roasts its own, and the line out the door on a Saturday morning is part of the experience.

📍 Nassau St · $ · Opens early

American

Agricola

Communal tables, wood-fired cooking, and plates built to share — roasted carrots, crispy trout salad, that register. Modern American with enough international technique to keep it interesting.

📍 Downtown Princeton · $$$ · Dinner

ItalianSince 1991

Teresa Caffe

Palmer Square's Italian caffe from the Momo restaurant family — pastas, pizzas, and a reliably busy room since 1991.

📍 Palmer Square · $$ · Lunch & Dinner

MediterraneanSince 1996

Mediterra

Mediterranean restaurant and taverna on Hulfish Street from the same family behind Teresa Caffe, celebrating 30 years in 2026.

📍 29 Hulfish St · $$$ · Lunch & Dinner

Ice CreamFamous

The Bent Spoon

The award-winning farm-to-spoon ice cream shop on the square — small batches, local ingredients, and flavors that change constantly.

📍 35 Palmer Sq W · $ · Daily

French

Kristine's

Classic French cooking — with vegan and vegetarian options — a few steps up Witherspoon from the square.

📍 51 Witherspoon St · $$$ · Brunch & Dinner

After Dark

Nightlife

TavernLandmark

The Alchemist & Barrister

Known around town as the "Cheers of Princeton" — a landmark tavern in a historic building, with live local bands on Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday nights. One of the few Princeton bars that can stay open until 2 AM.

📍 28 Witherspoon St · Open until 2 AM

Brewpub

Triumph Brewing Company

A Nassau Street microbrewery with a genuinely mixed room — students, locals and the corporate crowd — and a rotating live-music calendar spread through the week rather than parked on the weekend.

📍 Nassau St · Open until 2 AM

Dive Bar

Ivy Inn

Pool tables, cheap drinks, friendly bartenders and music that turns up without much warning. The least polished of Princeton's late-license bars, which is exactly why people are loyal to it.

📍 Princeton · Open until 2 AM

Historic Pub

Yankee Doodle Tap Room

The Nassau Inn's storied tap room — a Princeton gathering place with roots back to 1756, under the famous Norman Rockwell mural.

📍 10 Palmer Sq E · Until 10 PM

Shops & Screens

Record StoreWorld-Class

Princeton Record Exchange

Buying and selling music and movies since 1980, with an inventory north of 100,000 LPs, CDs and DVDs that turns over constantly. Routinely named among the leading independent record stores in the world — people drive hours for it.

📍 Princeton · Open daily

Cinema

Princeton Garden Theatre

Owned by the University and run by Renew Theaters — first-run films of real artistic quality, plus classics, foreign language programming and Saturday kids' matinees. NJ.com named it the best movie theater in New Jersey.

📍 Nassau St · Daily screenings

HotelHistoric

The Nassau Inn

The anchor of Palmer Square since the square was built, and the reason the whole block is laid out the way it is. Worth walking through even if you are not staying.

📍 Palmer Square · Est. 1937
