Northern LBI · Long Beach Township

Loveladies & North Beach

The island's quietest miles: three Long Beach Township enclaves tucked between Barnegat Light, Harvey Cedars, and Surf City — Loveladies (named for 19th-century gunning-club owner Thomas Lovelady), the bayside lagoons of High Bar Harbor, and dune-backed North Beach. No stores, no bars, no boardwalk — just architect-designed beach houses, high dunes, and a nationally respected arts foundation.

The Arts Anchor

Arts & SciencesMust-See

Long Beach Island Foundation of the Arts & Sciences

Founded in 1948 by sculptor Boris Blai — a Rodin student and founding dean of Temple's Tyler School of Art — on 22.8 acres of dune and marsh. Galleries, classes and workshops, marine-science programs, summer camps, and the annual national juried "Works on Paper" show (28th edition in 2026).

📍 120 Long Beach Blvd, Loveladies · (609) 494-1241 · Open daily, year-round

Architecture

The Loveladies House Tour (of the Streets)

After the 1962 storm leveled the north end, Loveladies rebuilt bold — it's now LBI's open-air gallery of modernist and contemporary beach architecture. LBIF even runs speaker programs on the modernist houses.

📍 Lagoon and ocean lanes off the Boulevard · Look, don't trespass

North Beach's Dune Beaches

Some of the island's highest dunes and least-crowded guarded sand — street-end walkovers, no commercial strip behind you, Barnegat Lighthouse on the horizon.

📍 Street ends off Long Beach Blvd · LBT beach badge required

Bay

High Bar Harbor

A boater's pocket of lagoon-front homes on the peninsula jutting into Barnegat Bay beside Barnegat Light — calm water, docks, and back-bay sunsets.

📍 Off the Boulevard at the island's north end · Residential

Practical Notes

No Shops, No Restaurants

There is deliberately nothing commercial here. Stock up before you arrive, or head minutes north to Barnegat Light (Kubel's, Viking Village) or south to Harvey Cedars and Surf City.

Parking Is Scarce

Beach access is by street-end walkovers with very limited parking. Bike the Boulevard's shoulder lane or ride the free LBI Shuttle instead.

The Name

Loveladies is no romance-novel invention — it comes from Thomas Lovelady, whose small gunning island sat just offshore in the 1800s. The name stuck to the whole stretch.

Check the LBIF Calendar

Exhibitions, lectures, kids' camps, tennis and pickleball, even mahjong — the foundation's seasonal catalog is the events list for this end of the island. lbifoundation.org has current dates.

Quick Tips

🖼

LBIF's gallery openings are free — the best way to see inside the campus Boris Blai built.

📷

The Boulevard through North Beach has the island's most photogenic dune line — go at golden hour.

🦞

Dinner plan: badge the beach here all day, then drive 5 minutes to Viking Village in Barnegat Light for the day-boat catch.
