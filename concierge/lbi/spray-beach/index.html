Southern LBI · Long Beach Township

Spray Beach & the Terraces

The mellow mile and a half just north of Beach Haven: five Long Beach Township communities — Beach Haven Terrace, Beach Haven Gardens, Bay Vista, Spray Beach, and North Beach Haven — where quiet rental blocks hide a genuine little restaurant row on the Boulevard around 132nd Street and one of the island's only truly oceanfront hotels.

Dining & Nightlife

TavernIconic Spot

The Terrace Tavern

A Terrace fixture in a tavern room dating to 1950 — local seafood, 20 beers on tap, live music from 5 PM, and happy hour Sunday–Thursday 3–5.

📍 13201 Long Beach Blvd, Beach Haven Terrace · Full bar · Live music

Oysters & SeafoodYear-Round

Delaware Avenue Oyster House & Bar

Family-owned oyster bar born out of a post-Sandy rebuild, right next door to the Terrace Tavern — raw bar and tapas-style plates, open seven days year-round.

📍 13211 Long Beach Blvd, Beach Haven Terrace · Full bar · Year-round

Beachfront DiningOn the Sand

Drift Beach Bar & Restaurant

The Spray Beach Hotel's beachfront restaurant — one of the only places on LBI where you eat and drink with the ocean directly in front of you, breakfast through dinner.

📍 Spray Beach Oceanfront Hotel, 200 E 24th St · Seasonal

The Five Communities

Beach Haven Terrace

The northern edge of this stretch and its commercial heart — the Terrace Tavern and Delaware Avenue Oyster House sit side by side on the Boulevard around 132nd Street.

Beach Haven Gardens & Bay Vista

Residential blocks ocean-to-bay in the middle of the stretch — Bay Vista is the small bayside pocket with lagoon-front homes.

Spray Beach

The namesake community around the E 20s streets, anchored by the oceanfront hotel that has carried the Spray Beach name for generations.

North Beach Haven

The southernmost township blocks, running seamlessly into Beach Haven borough — closest to Fantasy Island and the borough's dining district.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Long Beach Township, between The Dunes and Beach Haven borough — Beach Haven Terrace, Beach Haven Gardens, Bay Vista, Spray Beach, North Beach Haven

🏖

Beach badges: Long Beach Township badges — valid on all township beaches, Loveladies to Holgate.

🚗

Parking: Free street parking at most beach entrances; fills late morning in peak summer.

🚴

By bike: Flat, quiet side streets — Beach Haven's restaurants and Fantasy Island are minutes away.

🚌

Shuttle: The free LBI Shuttle stops along the Boulevard all summer.

Quick Tips

🦪

Delaware Avenue Oyster House is one of the few LBI rooms open all winter — a reliable off-season table.

🎶

Terrace Tavern's music starts at 5 PM — an early-evening set before the late rooms get going.

🌊

Drift is on the sand — sunset dinner there beats waiting for a window table anywhere else on the island.
