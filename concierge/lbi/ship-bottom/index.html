Ship Bottom

Where the causeway meets the island. The first and last town you pass through on every LBI trip — a low-key, unpretentious community that punches above its weight with a legendary brewery, excellent seafood, a great local bar scene, and the island's most iconic kite festival.

Practical Notes

Gateway Position

Ship Bottom is the first town you reach crossing the causeway from Route 72. It's a natural stop for groceries, beer runs, and supplies before heading elsewhere on the island. Several grocery stores and the best off-sale liquor selection on LBI are here.

Supply Stop

Stock up here. Acme Markets, a ShopRite, multiple liquor stores, bait shops, surf shops, and hardware stores are all in Ship Bottom — conveniences that aren't as accessible in the smaller towns further up or down the island.

Beach Badges

Ship Bottom sells its own beach badges, valid on Ship Bottom beaches only. Purchase at the municipal building or at the beach entrance booths. Daily, weekly, and seasonal badges available.

Off-Season Vibe

More year-round residents than most LBI towns. Ship Bottom Brewery, The Arlington, and a handful of restaurants stay open through fall and winter. If you're visiting in the off-season, Ship Bottom has more life than the quieter residential towns.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Central LBI, where Route 72 causeway meets the island

🚗

Parking: Easier than southern towns. Municipal lot near the beach. Street parking on side streets.

🏖

Beach: Wide, clean ocean beach. Less crowded than Beach Haven in peak season.

🛒

Groceries: Best grocery selection on LBI — Acme Markets and ShopRite both here.

🍺

Liquor: Best off-sale liquor selection on the island for BYOB restaurants.
