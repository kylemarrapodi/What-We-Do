Ship Bottom

Where the causeway meets the island. The first and last town you pass through on every LBI trip — a low-key, unpretentious community that punches above its weight with a legendary brewery, excellent seafood, a great local bar scene, and the island's most iconic kite festival.

Bars & Breweries

Craft BeerBrewery

Ship Bottom Brewery

LBI's premier craft brewery and a genuine Shore destination. Rotating taps of house-brewed IPAs, stouts, lagers, and seasonal specialties. The taproom has an easy-going bayview vibe, regular live music, and food from rotating food trucks. Growler fills and canned beer to go. One of the best brewery taprooms in the region — worth the trip by itself.

📍 Ship Bottom · Taproom open daily · Food trucks on weekends

BarLocal Favorite

The Arlington

A beloved Ship Bottom bar that's been a staple of LBI nightlife for decades. No pretense, strong drinks, and a crowd that ranges from sun-burned beachgoers to year-round locals. Live music on weekends, great for watching sports, and one of the few bars on LBI with a genuinely local feel. The kind of place you go once and keep coming back.

📍 Ship Bottom · Full bar · Live music weekends

Dining

SeafoodMust-Try

Ship Bottom Shellfish Co.

The freshest local shellfish on the island, sourced directly from Barnegat Bay. Raw bar with local oysters, clams, and shrimp. Steamed lobsters, crab claws, and market-price fish fillets. A no-frills, dockside operation that reminds you where you are. Get there early — they sell out. Outdoor picnic table seating.

📍 Bay side, Ship Bottom · Cash preferred · Seasonal hours

American

Show Place Ice Cream

The iconic Ship Bottom ice cream institution — a LBI rite of passage for decades of summer visitors. Dozens of flavors, generous portions, and a line that snakes down the block on summer evenings. Old-fashioned counter service and a classic Jersey Shore ice cream parlor vibe. Don't skip it.

📍 Long Beach Blvd, Ship Bottom · Cash · Seasonal

Pizza

LBI Pizza & Pasta

Reliable, family-friendly pizza and Italian classics that hit the spot after a day at the beach. Whole pies, slices, pasta dishes, subs, and salads. Quick service and solid value — the kind of place you return to every trip. Delivery available to nearby rentals in summer.

📍 Ship Bottom · Dine in & delivery · Open late in season

Breakfast

Uncle Will's Pancake House

A long-running Ship Bottom breakfast institution. Stacks of pancakes, fluffy omelets, egg sandwiches, and bottomless coffee. A beloved LBI morning ritual for families and regulars. Gets busy on weekend mornings — worth the short wait. Cash and card accepted.

📍 Ship Bottom · Breakfast & lunch · Seasonal

Events

Annual FestivalOctober

LBI Kite Festival

The largest kite festival on the Jersey Shore, held annually on the Ship Bottom beach in October. Hundreds of kites — from single-line beginner kites to massive show kites and elaborate artistic designs — fill the sky over the ocean. Demonstrations, competitions, vendors, and kite flying for all ages. A stunning visual spectacle and the signature Ship Bottom event of the year.

📍 Ship Bottom beach · October · Free · All ages

Bay ActivitySummer

Bay Fishing & Crabbing

Ship Bottom's bay-side location makes it a prime spot for recreational fishing and crabbing in Manahawkin Bay. Flounder, weakfish, and blue crabs are common catches from the bay docks and bridges. Several bait-and-tackle shops on the boulevard supply everything you need. No boat required — land-based crabbing spots abound.

📍 Manahawkin Bay docks · NJ fishing license required

Practical Notes

Gateway Position

Ship Bottom is the first town you reach crossing the causeway from Route 72. It's a natural stop for groceries, beer runs, and supplies before heading elsewhere on the island. Several grocery stores and the best off-sale liquor selection on LBI are here.

Supply Stop

Stock up here. Acme Markets, a ShopRite, multiple liquor stores, bait shops, surf shops, and hardware stores are all in Ship Bottom — conveniences that aren't as accessible in the smaller towns further up or down the island.

Beach Badges

Ship Bottom sells its own beach badges, valid on Ship Bottom beaches only. Purchase at the municipal building or at the beach entrance booths. Daily, weekly, and seasonal badges available.

Off-Season Vibe

More year-round residents than most LBI towns. Ship Bottom Brewery, The Arlington, and a handful of restaurants stay open through fall and winter. If you're visiting in the off-season, Ship Bottom has more life than the quieter residential towns.

Need to Know

🛒

Groceries: Best grocery selection on LBI — Acme Markets and ShopRite both here.

🍺

Liquor: Best off-sale liquor selection on the island for BYOB restaurants.
