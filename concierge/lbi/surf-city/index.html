Surf City

The geographic and social center of Long Beach Island. A walkable, vibrant town with a strong surfing culture, LBI's most celebrated bayside hotel and beach club, Saturday farmers markets, excellent casual dining, and a bay beach that's a summer afternoon favorite for families.

Dining & Drinks

Tacos & BarLocal Favorite

Scojos

The original LBI taco joint and one of the most beloved casual spots on the island. Creative fish tacos, shrimp tacos, and a menu of shareable plates that pairs perfectly with a cold margarita. Loud, fun, packed in summer — exactly what it should be. Outdoor seating when the weather cooperates. Early-evening crowds spill out onto the deck.

📍 Long Beach Blvd, Surf City · Full bar · Seasonal

Seafood

Yellow Fin Restaurant

A consistent Surf City favorite for solid seafood in a relaxed atmosphere. Menu features daily fish specials, whole belly clam strips, seafood pasta, and reliable classics. BYOB. Outdoor deck seating with bay views. Good value for the quality — one of the better options on the mid-island for a sit-down dinner.

📍 Surf City · BYOB · Seasonal dinner service

Coffee

Surf City Coffee

A go-to morning stop for Surf City regulars. Quality espresso drinks, cold brew, and a solid selection of pastries and breakfast sandwiches. Unpretentious and quick — exactly what you need before the farmers market or a beach day. Small outdoor seating area. Gets busy but moves fast.

📍 Surf City · Mornings · Seasonal hours

Bar

Bird & Betty's

Craft cocktails, a thoughtful beer and wine list, and bar food that's actually worth ordering. A more grown-up alternative to the louder beach bars, with a warm neighborhood-bar atmosphere. Popular with the Surf City local crowd and a welcome change of pace from the typical Shore bar. Live music on select nights.

📍 Surf City · Full bar · Seasonal

Landmarks

HotelBeach Club

Surf City Hotel & Beach Club

LBI's most celebrated gathering spot — a bayside hotel with an outdoor beach club that defines the Surf City summer. The pool deck, tiki bar, and live music stage face the bay for spectacular sunset views. Rooms book out months in advance for peak summer. Even if you're not staying, the beach club bar is open to guests and walk-ins on a space-available basis. Sunday funday at the Beach Club is a summer ritual.

📍 Barnegat Ave, Surf City · Seasonal · Book early

Bay BeachFamily Favorite

Bay Beach & Kayak Launch

Surf City's bay beach is calm, warm, and shallow — perfect for young children and for launching paddleboards and kayaks into Barnegat Bay. The gradual sandy bottom extends far out at low tide. A popular late-afternoon spot when the ocean beach gets crowded or windy. Kayak rentals available nearby from Memorial Day through Labor Day.

📍 Bay side, Surf City · Free access · Rentals available

Market

Saturday Farmers Market

Weekly outdoor market held every Saturday from June through October at the Surf City Municipal Complex. Local produce, fresh-cut flowers, Jersey honey, artisan baked goods, homemade jams, handmade crafts, and prepared food vendors. A great Saturday morning ritual before the beach. Draws regulars from all over the island.

📍 Municipal Complex · Sat 8am–1pm · June–October

Surfing

Surf Culture & Ocean Beach

Surf City lives up to its name — the beach has a legitimate surf culture with consistent waves that attract shortboarders, longboarders, and beginner surfers. Multiple surf schools operate here in summer. The designated surf zone is well-organized and respected. Best surf in shoulder season (May, September–October) when summer crowds thin out.

📍 Ocean beach, Surf City · Lessons available · Badge required

Practical Notes

Central Location

Surf City is the mid-point of LBI — a 10-minute bike ride or short drive from almost anywhere on the island. It makes an ideal base for exploring the full island without committing to the southern or northern ends.

Beach Hotel Booking

The Surf City Hotel is one of the few true hotel options on LBI with a full beach club experience. It books out for July and August by early spring. If you want a summer weekend here, check availability in February or March.

Surf Lessons

Multiple surf schools operate out of Surf City's beach. Beginner group lessons are widely available. Book in advance for summer weekends. The beach has a designated surf zone separate from the swimming areas.

Biking

Surf City is the best town on LBI for biking — flat, compact, and perfectly situated for rides north toward Harvey Cedars or south toward Ship Bottom on the island-spanning bike path along the bay side of Long Beach Blvd.

Need to Know

Parking: Street parking throughout. Less crowded than Beach Haven. Municipal lot near the beach.

🏖

🏄

Surf conditions: Best waves on the island. Designated surf zones marked with flags.

🚴

Bike path: Connects north to Harvey Cedars and south to Ship Bottom. Best biking on LBI.
