Central LBI · Long Beach Township

Brant Beach & the Mid-Island

The long central stretch of the island that most visitors drive through — and shouldn't. Seven Long Beach Township communities run down the Boulevard from Ship Bottom to the Terrace: Brant Beach, Beach Haven Crest, Brighton Beach, Peahala Park, Beach Haven Park, Haven Beach, and The Dunes. This is the township's civic heart — town hall, the bayfront at 68th Street, and the only room on the island with live music seven nights a week.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Long Beach Township's central section — Ship Bottom border south to the Terrace, communities north to south: Brant Beach, Beach Haven Crest, Brighton Beach, Peahala Park, Beach Haven Park, Haven Beach, The Dunes

🏖

Beach badges: Long Beach Township badges — valid on all township beaches from Loveladies to Holgate. Sold at town hall and beach entrances.

🚗

Parking: Free street-end parking at most beaches; free lot at Bayview Park (68th St).

🚌

Shuttle: The free LBI Shuttle runs the Boulevard through the whole stretch all summer.

📱

Township recreation: (609) 361-6697 · longbeachtownship.com

Quick Tips

🌅

Bayview Park faces west — the 68th Street bay beach is the mid-island's best free sunset seat.

🎸

Nardi's is the island's late room — check the band schedule and grab the pink bus instead of driving.

🥞

Blue Water Cafe's brunch line moves fastest before 9 AM on summer weekends.
