Getting to LBI

One road in: Route 72 Causeway at Ship Bottom. From NYC: ~90 min. From Philadelphia: ~75 min. From Atlantic City: ~40 min. No public transit to the island — bring your own wheels or rideshare to the bridge.

What Makes LBI Different

🚫

No Boardwalk, No Chains

Unlike Asbury Park or Atlantic City, LBI has no main boardwalk and virtually no chain restaurants or shops. Every business is independently owned.

🍷

BYOB Culture

Many of LBI's best restaurants are BYOB. Pick up a bottle at a local liquor store on your way in — it's part of the culture and keeps the vibe relaxed.

🏷

Beach Badges Required

All LBI beaches require paid badges (except some off-season days). Prices vary by town. Season badges are worth it if you're staying a week or more.

🚌

Free Island Shuttle

The LBI Shuttle runs along Long Beach Blvd every 5–20 minutes, 10am–10pm all summer. Leave the car at the house — it's free.

🌊

Ocean vs. Bay

Atlantic Ocean on the east for surf and swimming. Barnegat Bay on the west for kayaking, crabbing, and calm-water boating. Two completely different experiences on one narrow island.

🐟

Working Fishing Village

Barnegat Light's Viking Village is one of the busiest commercial fishing ports on the East Coast. Fresh scallops and clams pulled from the water that morning — genuinely.
