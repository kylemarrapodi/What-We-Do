Southernmost LBI

Holgate

The wild, untouched southern tip of LBI. Where the paved road ends, three miles of federally protected wilderness beach begins — no lifeguards, no concessions, just surf, piping plovers, and empty dunes stretching south toward Atlantic City.

The Wilderness

Wildlife RefugeMust-See

Forsythe Wilderness Beach

Edwin B. Forsythe National Wildlife Refuge. Over 400 acres of pristine barrier beach, high dunes, and tidal salt marsh designated a federal Wilderness Area in 1975. No development, no facilities — just the most unspoiled stretch of beach on the Jersey Shore.

📍 End of Barnegat Ave · Free · Sunrise to sunset

FishingLocal Favorite

Surf Fishing

Among the best surf fishing on the entire East Coast. Striped bass, bluefish, weakfish, and flounder run the Holgate tip seasonally. The point where the bay meets the ocean creates exceptional structure for feeding fish. Serious anglers come specifically for Holgate.

📍 Ocean & bay sides · NJ fishing license required

Wildlife

Birding & Wildlife

One of New Jersey's largest breeding grounds for federally threatened piping plovers. Also nests American oystercatchers, black skimmers, and least terns. Critical link on the Atlantic Flyway migration route — exceptional for fall shorebird watching.

📍 Entire refuge · Best: spring nesting & fall migration

Beach Driving

4WD Beach Access

One of the few NJ beaches where you can drive on the sand — but only September 1 through March 31. Vehicles must obtain a beach buggy permit from Long Beach Township and stay seaward of the berm crest. 4WD and proper tire deflation required.

📍 Permitted Sep 1–Mar 31 · LBT permit required · 4WD only

Clamming Trail

Bay Activity

Holgate Clamming Trail

A 0.4-mile bay-side trail open for clamming from September 1 through March 15. Boat landing permitted. Hard clams (quahogs) and soft-shell clams (steamers) are commonly found. A classic Jersey Shore experience — bring a rake and a bucket.

📍 Bay side, south end · Sep 1–Mar 15 · NJ shellfish license required

Practical Notes

No Facilities

No restrooms, no concessions, no lifeguards in the refuge. Bring everything you need — water, sunscreen, food. Nearest services are in Beach Haven (~2 miles north).

Bird Nesting Season

April 1–August 31: beach driving prohibited and portions of the beach are closed to protect nesting birds. Respect the roped-off areas — fines apply.

Dining Nearby

Holgate has no restaurants or bars. Head north to Beach Haven for dining — a 10-minute drive or bike ride along the boulevard.

Best Time to Visit

Fall (September–November) is ideal: beach driving opens, crowds disappear, fishing peaks, and shorebird migration is underway. Spring is excellent for birding before nesting restrictions.
