Harvey Cedars

LBI's most beloved small town. A quiet, residential community with an outsized reputation for exceptional food, a legendary shellfish company, a celebrated coffee roaster, and the widest, most pristine beach on the island. The kind of place regulars guard jealously and first-timers never stop talking about.

Practical Notes

Book Restaurants Early

Black Eyed Susan's books out weeks in advance for summer Saturdays. If it's a dining priority, check availability before you even book your rental. Birdy's and Harvey Cedars Shellfish Co. are more walk-in friendly but can have waits on summer evenings.

Quiet by Design

Harvey Cedars is a quiet residential community that attracts visitors who want that. There are no arcades, no big nightlife scene, no crowds. If you're here for the beach, the food, and the bay — it's perfect. If you need entertainment, Beach Haven is a short drive south.

BYOB Culture

Harvey Cedars' best restaurants are BYOB. Stock up on wine and beer at the Ship Bottom liquor stores before heading north. No corkage fee at most spots. White wine in the fridge, red out on the deck — plan accordingly.

Getting There

About 12 miles north of the causeway — a 20-minute drive up Long Beach Blvd in normal traffic. By bike, the bay-side path from Surf City makes for a pleasant ride. Barnegat Light is just 4 miles further north if you want to keep going.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Northern LBI, Harvey Cedars Borough

🚗

Parking: Easy street parking in most areas. Not a problem outside peak weekends.

🏖

Beach badges: Required on ocean beaches. Purchase at beach entrance booths.

🍷

BYOB tip: Stock up before you arrive — no liquor stores in Harvey Cedars itself.

📅

Best season: Summer and early fall. The beach is exceptional. Some restaurants reduce hours post–Labor Day.
