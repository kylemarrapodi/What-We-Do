Harvey Cedars

LBI's most beloved small town. A quiet, residential community with an outsized reputation for exceptional food, a legendary shellfish company, a celebrated coffee roaster, and the widest, most pristine beach on the island. The kind of place regulars guard jealously and first-timers never stop talking about.

Dining

American BYOBBest on LBI

Black Eyed Susan's

The most acclaimed restaurant on Long Beach Island — full stop. Creative American cuisine with a focus on fresh, local ingredients, executed with a level of care and skill that would impress in any major city. The seasonal menu changes constantly. BYOB. Tables are coveted: reservations required weeks in advance for peak summer. A mandatory dining experience for any serious LBI visitor.

📍 Harvey Cedars · BYOB · Reservations weeks in advance

SeafoodLocal Legend

Harvey Cedars Shellfish Co.

A Harvey Cedars institution operating since 1978. Fresh, direct-from-the-bay seafood at honest prices — local oysters, hard clams, steamed crabs, lobster, and daily fish. BYOB. The deck seating on warm evenings is perfect. A cash operation (ATM on site) with an unmistakable Shore character that no upscale restaurant can replicate. Order the steamed crabs and stay a while.

📍 Harvey Cedars · BYOB · Cash preferred · Seasonal

CoffeeMust-Visit

Marigold Coffee Roasters

Arguably the best coffee on LBI — a genuine specialty roaster with a warm, bright café that's become a Harvey Cedars anchor. Single-origin pour-overs, beautifully made lattes, and exceptional cold brew. The pastries are excellent. A reliable morning ritual for locals and visitors who know their coffee. Small but never feels rushed.

📍 Harvey Cedars · Morning–afternoon · Seasonal hours

American

Birdy's

A newer addition to the Harvey Cedars dining scene that's quickly earned a loyal following. Approachable American menu — elevated bar food, excellent burgers, and a selection of fresh fish dishes. Full bar. Casual and comfortable, with a back patio that catches the bay breeze on summer evenings. A welcome addition to a town already blessed with great food options.

📍 Harvey Cedars · Full bar · Seasonal

Beach & Outdoors

BeachBest on LBI

Harvey Cedars Beach

The widest, most expansive beach on LBI — a broad sweep of clean sand with generous dune protection and clear, clean water. Less crowded than Beach Haven even in peak season because fewer visitors make it this far north. The beach has a calm, unhurried quality that matches the town's character. Worth the extra drive from the causeway.

📍 Ocean side, Harvey Cedars · Badge required · Lifeguards in season

Market

Friday Farmers Market

Weekly Friday market at Veterans Memorial Park throughout summer. Local growers, specialty food producers, fresh-baked goods, handmade crafts, and prepared food vendors. Smaller and more intimate than the Surf City Saturday market, but the quality of vendors is exceptional. A casual, low-key way to start a Harvey Cedars Friday.

📍 Veterans Memorial Park · Fri morning · June–September

Practical Notes

Book Restaurants Early

Black Eyed Susan's books out weeks in advance for summer Saturdays. If it's a dining priority, check availability before you even book your rental. Birdy's and Harvey Cedars Shellfish Co. are more walk-in friendly but can have waits on summer evenings.

Quiet by Design

Harvey Cedars is a quiet residential community that attracts visitors who want that. There are no arcades, no big nightlife scene, no crowds. If you're here for the beach, the food, and the bay — it's perfect. If you need entertainment, Beach Haven is a short drive south.

BYOB Culture

Harvey Cedars' best restaurants are BYOB. Stock up on wine and beer at the Ship Bottom liquor stores before heading north. No corkage fee at most spots. White wine in the fridge, red out on the deck — plan accordingly.

Getting There

About 12 miles north of the causeway — a 20-minute drive up Long Beach Blvd in normal traffic. By bike, the bay-side path from Surf City makes for a pleasant ride. Barnegat Light is just 4 miles further north if you want to keep going.

Need to Know

Location: Northern LBI, Harvey Cedars Borough

🚗

Parking: Easy street parking in most areas. Not a problem outside peak weekends.

🏖

Beach badges: Required on ocean beaches. Purchase at beach entrance booths.

🍷

BYOB tip: Stock up before you arrive — no liquor stores in Harvey Cedars itself.

📅

Best season: Summer and early fall. The beach is exceptional. Some restaurants reduce hours post–Labor Day.
