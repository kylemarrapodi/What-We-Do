Barnegat Light

The iconic tip of LBI, anchored by the 172-foot red-and-white lighthouse that has guided ships through Barnegat Inlet since 1859. A historic fishing village with a working fleet at Viking Village, extraordinary restaurants, and beaches so wide the ocean feels a mile away. The most atmospheric town on the island.

Barnegat Lighthouse State Park

New Jersey's most iconic lighthouse — "Old Barney" — has stood at the northern tip of LBI since 1859. Climb 217 steps through the restored interior to the top for sweeping views of Barnegat Inlet, the bay, and the open Atlantic. The surrounding state park offers jetty fishing, a nature trail through maritime forest, and beach access at the inlet. A New Jersey landmark by any measure.

📍 N. Broadway, Barnegat Light · Small admission fee · Open daily in season

Working PortFishing

Viking Village

A genuine working fishing village on the bay side — one of the last authentic commercial fishing ports on the Jersey Shore. Watch the fleet come in each morning to unload fresh catch. Several retail fish shops sell direct from the boats at the docks. Walk the boardwalk alongside the working vessels, fish markets, and crab shacks. Head Boats (party fishing boats) depart daily for bay and offshore trips. A living piece of LBI maritime history.

📍 19th St, bay side · Free to walk · Head boats depart daily

Jetty Fishing

Barnegat Inlet Jetty

The north and south rock jetties flanking Barnegat Inlet are legendary among surf fishermen. Striped bass, bluefish, and weakfish run the inlet rips seasonally. The jetty walk at the state park offers a dramatic view of the inlet and the open ocean beyond. One of the most visually striking spots on LBI at any time of year — especially during a northeast swell.

📍 Barnegat Light State Park · NJ fishing license required

BeachWidest on Shore

Barnegat Light Beach

The broadest ocean beach on LBI — some sections approach 900 feet from dune to waterline at low tide. The result of the offshore sand shoals that historically made this inlet treacherous for navigation. Clean, clear water with light crowds outside of peak season. Free beach access in the state park. Badge-required beach access at the southern municipal sections.

📍 Ocean side · State park section free · Municipal section badge required

Dining

American BYOBLegendary

Kubel's

A Barnegat Light institution that belongs in the pantheon of great Shore restaurants. Kubel's has been feeding LBI families for decades from its no-frills dining room — excellent seafood, reliable American classics, and a warm familiarity that no new restaurant can manufacture. BYOB. Gets crowded on summer evenings. Go for the whole flounder or the crab cakes. The kind of place grandparents bring grandchildren to the restaurant they first came to as children.

📍 Barnegat Light · BYOB · Seasonal · Dinner only

Seafood BYOBTop Rated

Wally's Restaurant

Creative seafood in a relaxed, well-designed dining room. Wally's has developed a strong following for its thoughtful preparations of local fish and shellfish, rotating seasonal menu, and warm service. BYOB. Reservations recommended in summer. One of the best all-around dining experiences in Barnegat Light — particularly strong on the fish dishes sourced directly from the Viking Village fleet.

📍 Barnegat Light · BYOB · Reservations recommended

WaterfrontBar

Daymark Restaurant & Bar

A newer addition to the Barnegat Light dining scene with a prime waterfront location and full bar — the only place with bay views and cocktails in town. The menu runs from casual lunch bites to proper dinner plates, all with a fresh seafood emphasis. Outdoor deck seating on summer evenings is among the best settings for a meal anywhere on LBI. Sunset over the bay from here is something to plan around.

📍 Bay side, Barnegat Light · Full bar · Waterfront deck

Seafood Market

Viking Village Fish Market

Buy your seafood direct from the boats at the Viking Village docks. The freshest product on LBI — fillets, whole fish, live lobsters, and local shellfish sold at dockside prices. Morning arrivals bring the best selection. Bring a cooler. The retail shops along the Viking Village boardwalk also carry smoked fish, chowder, and prepared seafood for the trip home.

📍 Viking Village docks · Morning for best selection · Cash preferred

Practical Notes

Lighthouse Timing

Arrive at the lighthouse by 9am on summer weekends to skip lines. The climb is 217 steps — sturdy shoes are recommended. Views from the top encompass the full island to the south, the inlet, and on clear days, the Atlantic City skyline to the southwest.

End of the Road

Barnegat Light is the northern terminus of LBI — the road ends here. That finality gives it a distinct character: quieter, more purposeful, more local. You drive all the way up the island to get here, and it's always worth the trip.

Head Boat Fishing

Viking Village runs head boats (party fishing vessels) for half-day and full-day offshore trips. Book in advance for summer departures. Tackle and bait provided. No experience necessary — a classic Jersey Shore fishing experience.

Off-Season Visit

Barnegat Light in October or November is one of the most atmospheric places on the Jersey Shore. The commercial fleet is still active, a few restaurants remain open, the lighthouse and state park are uncrowded, and the inlet in a northeast wind is a spectacle. Worth the drive.
