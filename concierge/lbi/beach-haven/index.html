Beach Haven

The commercial and cultural heart of LBI. A Victorian seaside town with 150 years of history, the island's best dining scene, an iconic amusement park, professional summer theater, and the biggest festivals on the Shore. Beach Haven is where LBI comes alive.

HopSauce Festival

Craft beer meets artisan hot sauce at the Taylor Ave Waterfront. 50+ local and regional breweries, dozens of small-batch hot sauce makers, live music, and food vendors. One of LBI's most popular food festivals — tickets sell out. Held at the bayfront park.

Wed

Concerts on the Green

Free Wednesday evening concerts at the Centre Street stage throughout July and August. Genres rotate each week — classic rock, jazz, folk, beach pop. Bring a chair or blanket. Family-friendly and free. A Beach Haven summer tradition going back decades.

Coquina Jam Surf Competition

The premier surf competition on LBI, held at Beach Haven's ocean beach. Amateur and professional divisions, spectator-friendly beach access, and a festive atmosphere. Named for the coquina clam shells that dot LBI's beaches. One of the signature surf events on the Jersey Shore.

LBI Film Festival

Five days of independent film screenings, filmmaker Q&As, and panel discussions across multiple Beach Haven venues. Attracts filmmakers from across the country. Evening screenings often sell out. The cultural anchor of LBI's fall season, typically held in late September.

Chowderfest

LBI's biggest annual event. Thousands of visitors descend on the Taylor Ave Waterfront to sample clam chowder from dozens of restaurants and vendors competing for the best chowder on the Shore. Live music, beer garden, and a festive fall atmosphere. Held the first weekend of October — book lodging months in advance.

LBI Chefs Dinner Series

Multi-course collaborative dinners featuring LBI's best restaurant chefs cooking together in the off-season. Intimate ticketed events highlighting local seafood and regional ingredients. A celebration of LBI's serious culinary scene outside the summer frenzy.

Dining & Nightlife

Seafood BYOBTop Rated

Barnacle Bill's

A Beach Haven institution for fresh, unpretentious seafood at honest prices. Locals swear by the whole belly clams, steamed shrimp, and the lobster roll. Cash-friendly, no reservations, BYOB. Expect a wait on summer weekends — worth it. The quintessential LBI seafood experience.

📍 10th St & The Boulevard · BYOB · Seasonal

American BYOBLocal Favorite

The Black Whale

The go-to for creative American cuisine in a warm, cozy setting. Menu changes seasonally but consistently features excellent fish dishes, handmade pasta, and inventive appetizers. BYOB adds to the value. Reservations highly recommended in July and August. One of the best overall dining experiences on the island.

📍 Centre St · BYOB · Reservations recommended

Sushi BYOB

Beach Haven Sushi

Consistently excellent Japanese cuisine — surprising in a Shore town but genuinely outstanding. Fresh fish flown in daily, creative rolls, and solid traditional options. One of the harder tables to get on LBI without advance planning. BYOB. Open for dinner in season.

📍 Beach Haven · BYOB · Reservations recommended

BarIconic

The Ketch Bar & Grill

LBI's most iconic bar — a true Shore institution that's been a summer gathering spot for decades. Live music on weekends, cold beer, a full bar, and a crowd that's been coming back every summer. Casual food menu. Outdoor deck. The place where LBI summers begin and end for generations of island loyalists.

📍 Beach Haven · Full bar · Live music weekends

Breakfast

Parker's Garage Diner & Filling Station

Beloved breakfast and lunch spot occupying a converted vintage service garage. Great omelets, oversized pancakes, and inventive egg dishes. The retro-industrial décor is part of the charm. A weekend morning ritual for Beach Haven regulars. Expect a line on summer weekends — move early.

📍 Beach Haven · Breakfast & lunch · Cash & card

Bar & Grill

Tuckers Bar & Grill

A longstanding Beach Haven staple for classic bar food and a lively atmosphere. Wings, burgers, and sandwiches done right. Popular for watching sports, late-night eats, and a drink after the beach. Friendly staff who treat you like you've been coming for years — because many of the regulars have.

📍 Beach Haven · Full bar · Late kitchen

Where to Stay

Motel9.2 · Excellent

Sea Spray Motel

Family-owned motel a few blocks from the beach, with 52 rooms and efficiencies, a heated pool, and free parking right on the bay. Simple, comfortable, and walkable to the center of town — a longtime favorite for guests who want an easy, no-fuss LBI stay.

📍 2600 S. Bay Ave · Heated pool · Free parking

Attractions

IconicAmusement

Fantasy Island Amusement Park

LBI's beloved boardwalk-style amusement park, operating since 1984 at 750 N. Bay Ave. Free admission to the park — pay per ride. 18 rides including a classic carousel, Ferris wheel, bumper cars, and children's rides. Arcade games, skee-ball, and a full midway. Open Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day, evenings in summer.

📍 750 N. Bay Ave · Free admission · Seasonal

Water ParkFamily

Thundering Surf Waterpark

The Shore's premier waterpark since 1977, now operated by the Fantasy Island family. Features a FlowRider surf simulator, lazy river, water slides, and a dedicated kids' area. Located at 300 Taylor Ave adjacent to the bay. Admission required. Open daily in summer.

📍 300 Taylor Ave · Admission required · Summer daily

TheaterMust-See

Surflight Theatre

One of New Jersey's most respected professional summer theaters, operating continuously since 1950. Full Broadway-caliber productions — musicals, comedies, and dramas — performed in an intimate 344-seat venue on Engleside Ave. A true LBI institution. Pre-show dining packages available. Book in advance; popular productions sell out.

📍 Engleside Ave · Seasonal · Advance tickets recommended

History

LBI Historical Association Museum

Founded in 1976, the museum preserves the history of Long Beach Island through rotating exhibits, artifacts, historical photographs, and documents. Located at 129 Engleside Ave in a restored historic structure. Celebrating its 50th anniversary in 2026. Free admission. Open in season Tuesday–Sunday.

📍 129 Engleside Ave · Free · Tue–Sun in season

Architecture

Victorian Historic District

A remarkable collection of late 19th and early 20th century resort architecture stretching from 5th Street to Chatsworth Avenue. Ornate Queen Anne cottages, Colonial Revival hotels, and shingle-style summer homes line the shaded streets. Pick up a self-guided walking tour map from the Historical Museum. Best explored on foot or by bike.

📍 5th St to Chatsworth Ave · Self-guided walking tours

Bay Beach

Bay Village & Bayfront

The western bay side of Beach Haven features calm, warm, shallow water ideal for families with young children, kayaking, and paddleboarding. The bayfront park at the end of Centre Street has benches and a boat launch. The Thundering Surf area at Taylor Ave waterfront hosts the major festivals throughout summer and fall.

📍 Taylor Ave Waterfront · Bay beaches along the west side

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Southern LBI, Long Beach Township

🏖

Beach badges: Required on ocean beaches. Sold at beach entrances and borough hall.

🚗

Parking: Metered street parking. Municipal lots near the bay and Fantasy Island. Fills by 10am in peak summer.

🚴

By bike: Flat and very bikeable. Rentals available on the boulevard. Most restaurants and attractions within 10 minutes of each other.

🍷

BYOB culture: Most better restaurants are BYOB. Bring wine or beer — no corkage fee at most spots.

📅

Peak season: July 4–Labor Day. Shoulder season (June, Sep) offers calmer crowds and full services.

Quick Tips

⏰

Arrive at Fantasy Island after 5pm on weekdays to skip the biggest crowds and catch the evening glow.

🎭

Surflight books out weeks ahead for top productions. Check their schedule when you book your trip.

🍺

Chowderfest lodging books out 3–6 months in advance. Plan well ahead for the October festival.

🍽️

BYOB restaurants are typically the best tables on LBI. Stop at Wine & Spirits on the boulevard before dinner.
