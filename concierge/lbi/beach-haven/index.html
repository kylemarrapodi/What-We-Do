Beach Haven

The commercial and cultural heart of LBI. A Victorian seaside town with 150 years of history, the island's best dining scene, an iconic amusement park, professional summer theater, and the biggest festivals on the Shore. Beach Haven is where LBI comes alive.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Southern LBI — an independent borough since 1890, bordered by Long Beach Township's Spray Beach section to the north and Holgate to the south

🏖

Beach badges: Required on ocean beaches. Sold at beach entrances and borough hall.

🚗

Parking: Metered street parking. Municipal lots near the bay and Fantasy Island. Fills by 10am in peak summer.

🚴

By bike: Flat and very bikeable. Rentals available on the boulevard. Most restaurants and attractions within 10 minutes of each other.

🍷

BYOB culture: Most better restaurants are BYOB. Bring wine or beer — no corkage fee at most spots.

📅

Peak season: July 4–Labor Day. Shoulder season (June, Sep) offers calmer crowds and full services.

Quick Tips

⏰

Arrive at Fantasy Island after 5pm on weekdays to skip the biggest crowds and catch the evening glow.

🎭

Surflight books out weeks ahead for top productions. Check their schedule when you book your trip.

🍺

Chowderfest lodging books out 3–6 months in advance. Plan well ahead for the October festival.

🍽️

BYOB restaurants are typically the best tables on LBI. Stop at Wine & Spirits on the boulevard before dinner.
