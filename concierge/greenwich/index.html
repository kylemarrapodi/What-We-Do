Greenwich

Connecticut's first town off the New York line, settled in 1640 and still the front door of the Gold Coast. Life here runs from the luxury storefronts of Greenwich Avenue down to a working harbor on Long Island Sound, out to the beach loop at Tod's Point, and up through miles of stone-walled backcountry lanes. An art-and-science museum that finished a $67 million expansion in 2023, four Metro-North stations, and an express train that reaches Grand Central in about 50 minutes keep it equal parts New England town and Manhattan satellite.

Beach & Park

Greenwich Point Park (Tod's Point)

Beach, loop road and Sound views on a peninsula in Old Greenwich. Non-residents May 1–Oct 31 need a $10 day pass plus $40 vehicle pass, bought online in advance — no sales at the gate. Free access the rest of the year.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: The southwest corner of Connecticut, bordering Westchester County, NY to the west and Stamford to the east.

🚆

Getting there: Metro-North's New Haven Line stops at four stations in town — Greenwich, Cos Cob, Riverside and Old Greenwich. Express trains reach Grand Central in about 50 minutes.

🚗

Driving: I-95 and the Merritt Parkway both cross the town; US-1 (the Post Road) is the local spine.

🏖

Beaches: Town beaches are resident-card territory in season. Non-residents need advance-purchase day passes May 1–Oct 31 ($10/person + $40 parking) — there are no sales at the gate.

🏘

Villages: Greenwich is really a collection of them — downtown, Cos Cob, Riverside, Old Greenwich, Glenville and Byram each have their own centers.

Quick Tips

🎟

The Bruce Museum is free all day on Tuesdays — the expanded galleries without the $20 ticket.

🏖

Planning a beach day at Tod's Point? Buy the non-resident day pass online before you drive — the gate can't sell you one.

🌅

L'Escale's terrace faces the harbor — book ahead for an evening table in summer.

🥐

Saturday morning: farmers market at the Horseneck lot from 9:30, then walk the Avenue as the stores open.
