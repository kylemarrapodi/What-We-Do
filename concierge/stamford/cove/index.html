Cove / East Side

A transitional waterfront neighborhood between downtown and the Darien border — tidal wetlands, quiet coves, a strong artistic community, and the best Vietnamese food you'll find in Fairfield County.

Where to Eat

Dining

VietnameseBest in CT

Pho Vietnam

The original and still the best on East Main's Vietnamese corridor. Enormous bowls of hand-simmered pho, flawless spring rolls, and a consistently packed house seven days a week.

📍 East Main St · $ · Lunch & Dinner daily

VietnameseLocal Favorite

Saigon City

Vietnamese classics with a Cantonese twist — bún bò Huế, bánh mì, and the best cold noodle salads on the east side. BYOB, cash preferred, always excellent.

📍 East Main St · $ · Lunch & Dinner

Italian

Teresa's by the Cove

A beloved neighborhood Italian with a devoted local following. Handmade pasta, Sunday gravy, and a dining room that feels like a standing invitation to stay for hours.

📍 Cove Rd · $$ · Dinner Tue–Sun

BangladeshiHidden Gem

Kazi's Kitchen

Authentic Bangladeshi cuisine that's largely unknown outside the neighborhood — hilsa fish curry, bhuna gosht, and a paratha breakfast that should be on every Stamford must-eat list.

📍 East Main St · $ · Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Bakery

Cove Island Bakery

A small-batch artisan bakery supplying local restaurants and selling direct on weekends. Sourdough, pastries, and seasonal specials made with locally sourced ingredients.

📍 East Side · $ · Sat–Sun mornings only

PizzaLandmark

Colony Grill

The 1935 East Side tavern behind Connecticut's famous thin-crust "hot oil" pizza — still the original location.

📍 172 Myrtle Ave · $ · Lunch & Dinner daily

See & Do

Arts & Culture

GalleryMust Visit

Cove Arts Collective

A working artist collective in the East Side industrial corridor with open studios on the first Friday of every month. Painting, sculpture, ceramics, and printmaking all under one roof.

📍 East Main St · First Fridays 6–9 PM · Free

Community Art

East Side Mural Walk

Over a dozen large-scale murals commissioned from local and regional artists across the neighborhood's industrial facades. A self-guided 45-minute walk that changes every few years.

📍 East Main St corridor · Always open · Free

Music

Cove Island Music Series

Free outdoor concerts at Cove Island Park throughout summer — jazz, folk, and world music every Thursday evening with the tidal cove as a backdrop.

📍 Cove Island Park · Thu evenings · Jun–Aug · Free

Get Outside

Outdoors

Park & BeachMust Visit

Cove Island Park

83-acre waterfront park with a sandy beach on Long Island Sound, picnic areas, tennis courts, and one of the most peaceful sunset walks in Stamford. Summer beach access requires a resident permit.

📍 Cove Rd · Open daily · Resident beach pass (summer)

Wetlands

East Side Tidal Wetlands

A network of tidal marshes, coves, and inlets accessible by foot trail. Prime birding — herons, osprey, and shorebirds congregate year-round. One of the most underrated natural areas in Stamford.

📍 East Side shoreline · Open daily · Free

Kayaking

Cove Kayak Launch

A public kayak and canoe launch in the cove giving access to sheltered tidal creeks and out to the Sound. One of the most scenic paddles in Fairfield County.

📍 Cove Rd · Open daily · Free
