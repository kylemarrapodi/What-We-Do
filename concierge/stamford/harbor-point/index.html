Get Outside

Outdoors & Water

MarinaMust Visit

Brewer Yacht Haven Marina

Full-service marina with slips for visiting boaters, fuel dock, ship's store, and direct access to Long Island Sound. A working harbor in the middle of the city.

📍 Harbor Point · Open daily · Seasonal

Waterfront Walk

Harbor Point Waterfront Promenade

A scenic mile-long waterfront walk connecting the marina to the beaches. Benches, public art, fishing spots, and sweeping Sound views at every turn.

📍 Harbor Point · Open 24/7 · Free

EducationKids

SoundWaters Community Center

Marine education center on the harbor with sailing programs, kayak tours, environmental science classes, and hands-on Long Island Sound exhibits. Excellent for families.

📍 Harbor Point · Programs year-round · soundwaters.org

Kayaking

Kayak & Paddleboard Rentals

Seasonal kayak and SUP rentals at the marina for self-guided paddles along the harbor and out into the Sound. No experience required — staff provides a brief orientation.

📍 Harbor Point Marina · May–Oct · $$ per hour

Park

Harbor Point Green

A beautifully landscaped central lawn surrounded by restaurants and the marina. Home to outdoor fitness classes, food truck pop-ups, and concerts throughout summer.

📍 Harbor Point Center · Open daily · Free

Fishing

Harbor Fishing Pier

A public fishing pier at the south end of the waterfront walk. Striped bass, bluefish, and flounder are common catches. Open to the public year-round, no permit required.

📍 South Harbor Point · Open daily · Free
