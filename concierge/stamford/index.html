Stamford's beloved dockside seafood institution. 11 boat slips, outdoor patio, frozen drinks, and a dance band on the patio. Open 35+ years.

Arts & Culture

Venues & Institutions

Theatre
Palace Theatre
📍 61 Atlantic St · Downtown

Historic 1,600-seat theatre originally opened in 1927. Broadway musicals, concerts, and major performances. Recently renovated.

Cinema
Avon Theatre Film Center
📍 Bedford St · Downtown

Non-profit dedicated to classic, alternative, and art-house films since 1939. Restored in 2004. Two screens.

Museum
Stamford Museum & Nature Center
📍 39 Scofieldtown Rd · North Stamford

80+ acres with nature trails, planetarium, Heckscher Farm, and Bendel Mansion galleries. Family favorite year-round.

Theatre
Curtain Call
📍 1349 Newfield Ave · Springdale

Fairfield County's longest-running non-profit theatre. 12+ productions annually, annual free outdoor Shakespeare. 30,000+ patrons per year.

Gardens
Bartlett Arboretum
📍 151 Brookdale Rd · North Stamford

93-acre living classroom with 13 gardens, champion trees, native habitats, summer concerts, and the Honey Harvest Festival.

Gallery
Yankell Legacy Gallery
📍 61 Studio Court

Fine art gallery in the historic stone studio of Gutzon Borglum — the sculptor of Mount Rushmore — built in 1911. By appointment.

Get Outside

Parks & Outdoors

Park
Mill River Park
📍 Downtown

Stamford's "mini Central Park" — 19 miles of shoreline access, summer concerts, the Nissen Carousel, movie nights, and year-round programming.

Waterfront
Cove Island Park
📍 Shippan

Paved loop trail, beach access, marinas, and picnic areas on Long Island Sound. One of Stamford's most scenic parks.

Trails
Mianus River State Park
📍 North Stamford

Popular hiking and mountain biking. Mianus Maze Trail is a 5-mile moderate loop. Network of fire roads and single-track.
