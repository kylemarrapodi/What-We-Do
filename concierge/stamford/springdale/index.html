Neighborhood

Springdale

A dense, diverse, and walkable mid-city neighborhood built around the Springdale train station. Immigrant-owned restaurants, lively local commerce, and Stamford's most authentic multicultural street life.

Where to Eat

Dining

BrazilianHidden Gem

Braza Brazilian BBQ

Authentic Brazilian churrascaria with all-you-can-eat rodízio service — skewered meats carved tableside, fresh salad bar, and the best caipirinha in Fairfield County.

📍 Hope St · $$ · Dinner nightly

PeruvianLocal Favorite

El Charrito

A Springdale institution for 20+ years. Rotisserie chicken, ceviche, lomo saltado, and a loyal following that drives from all over Fairfield County. BYOB and always packed.

📍 Hope St · $ · Lunch & Dinner daily

Mexican

Taco Loco

No-frills, no-seats, no-nonsense Mexican food truck turned brick-and-mortar. The al pastor tacos are among the best in Connecticut. Cash only, always fresh.

📍 Hope St · $ · Lunch & Dinner

Indian

Swagat Indian Cuisine

Family-run North Indian restaurant with excellent biryanis, rich curries, and a lunch buffet that's become a local ritual. One of the best Indian kitchens in Fairfield County.

📍 Hope St · $$ · Lunch & Dinner

Dominican

La Hacienda Restaurant

Authentic Dominican and Puerto Rican cooking with mofongo, pernil, and sancocho that feel straight from the island. Loud, fun, and extremely filling.

📍 Springdale Ave · $ · Lunch & Dinner

ItalianLocal Favorite

Springdale Pizza & Restaurant

Old-school neighborhood pizza joint with crispy-bottomed pies, hearty red sauce pasta, and a regulars-only feel that's been going strong since the 1960s.

📍 Hope St · $ · Lunch & Dinner daily

Sports Bar

Vinny's Backyard

Family-friendly sports bar and grill with pizza, wings, and games on every TV.

📍 1078 Hope St · $$ · Dinner & Late Night

Pizza

Riko's Pizza

Thin-crust pizza spot across from the Springdale train station, part of a small CT-grown chain.

Get Outside

Outdoors

ParkMust Visit

Mianus River Park

A 400-acre network of wooded trails, the Mianus River gorge, and rocky outcroppings straddling the Stamford-Greenwich border. One of the finest nature preserves in lower Fairfield County.

📍 Mianus River Rd · Open dawn to dusk · Free

Park

Springdale Park

Community park with ball fields, a playground, picnic areas, and a well-used walking trail. The social hub of the neighborhood — lively on weekends from morning to dusk.

📍 Hope St · Open daily · Free

Trail

Rails to Trails Greenway

A paved multi-use trail on a converted rail corridor connecting Springdale to downtown and to Greenwich. Perfect for cycling, running, or a long walk through the city.

📍 Access near Springdale Station · Open daily · Free
