See & Do

Arts & Culture

TheatreLandmark

Palace Theatre

A beautifully restored 1927 performing arts landmark presenting Broadway tours, major concerts, comedy, and family shows year-round.

📍 Atlantic St · Box office Mon–Sat

CinemaLandmark

Avon Theatre Film Center

Stamford's only independent art-house cinema since 1939. Foreign films, indie features, documentaries, and special engagement screenings.

📍 Bedford St · Daily screenings

Community Theatre

Curtain Call Theatre

Stamford's premier community theatre presenting musicals, plays, and revues across three stages. Often surprisingly polished — a great affordable night out.

📍 Scofieldtown Rd · Productions year-round

Public Art

Columbus Park Installations

The city's rotating public art program turns Columbus Park into an open-air gallery. Sculptures, murals, and interactive installations change each season.

📍 Columbus Park · Always open · Free

Gallery

Yankell Legacy Gallery

A downtown gem showcasing contemporary and emerging artists. Monthly rotating exhibitions, artist talks, and an accessible price point.

📍 Main St · Tue–Sat 11–6 PM · Free

Performing Arts

Stamford Center for the Arts

The umbrella organization behind the Palace and Rich Forum — presenting a full season of music, dance, spoken word, and family programming.

📍 Atlantic St · Season Sep–May

Get Outside

Outdoors

ParkMust Visit

Mill River Park

Downtown's crown jewel — 12 acres of beautifully designed urban parkland with a carousel, skating ribbon, splash pad, and event lawn.

📍 Mill River Park · Open daily · Free

Plaza

Columbus Park

The heart of downtown — home to the weekly Farmers Market, the Italian Festival, public art, and the city's most beloved outdoor gathering spot.

📍 Columbus Blvd · Open daily · Free

Memorial

Veterans Memorial Park

A quiet, contemplative green space adjacent to downtown with permanent memorials, mature trees, and walking paths.

📍 Atlantic St · Open daily · Free

Waterfront

Mill River Greenway Trail

A multi-use path tracing the Mill River from downtown toward the harbor. Flat, well-lit, and increasingly popular with the after-work crowd.

📍 Access from Mill River Park · Open daily

SkatingSeasonal

Mill River Skating Ribbon

Stamford's most photogenic winter attraction — a winding outdoor skating ribbon through Mill River Park. Open December through February.

📍 Mill River Park · Dec–Feb · Admission req.

CarouselFamily

Stamford Town Center Carousel

The beloved carousel at Mill River Park — hand-painted horses, classic music, and a line of kids and adults every sunny weekend.

📍 Mill River Park · Seasonal · $
