Where to Eat

Dining

SeafoodWaterfront

The Water's Edge at Giovanni's

Stamford's most celebrated waterfront restaurant on Shippan Ave — panoramic Sound views, meticulous service, and a menu built around Connecticut seafood and prime cuts.

📍 Shippan Ave · $$$$ · Dinner Tue–Sun

CasualLocal Favorite

Shippan Landing Restaurant

Laid-back seafood spot right on the water with lobster rolls, clam strips, and ice-cold beer. A Shippan tradition since the 1970s and still the best casual waterfront meal in town.

📍 Shippan Landing · $$ · Lunch & Dinner (seasonal)

Breakfast

Shore & Country Café

A beloved neighborhood breakfast and lunch spot serving the Shippan community for decades. Classic American fare, cheerful service, and the kind of place where everyone knows your name.

📍 Shippan Ave · $ · Breakfast & Lunch daily

American

Harbor View Restaurant

Classic American comfort food with water views. Known for generous portions, weekend brunch, and a low-key atmosphere that feels distinctly un-Stamford in the best way.

📍 Shippan Point · $$ · Brunch & Dinner

ItalianTop Rated

Cafe Silvium

Family-owned Italian trattoria on Shippan Avenue, ranked among Stamford's very best restaurants.

Get Outside

Outdoors & Waterfront

BeachMust Visit

Shippan Point Beaches

Three resident-access beaches along the peninsula's Sound-facing shore. Calm waters, beautiful sunsets, and a peaceful atmosphere worlds away from the city just minutes north.

📍 Shippan Point Peninsula · Resident access required

SailingHistoric

Stamford Yacht Club

Founded in 1883, one of the oldest and most prestigious yacht clubs on Long Island Sound. Racing programs, junior sailing, and social events throughout the season.

📍 Shippan Point · Members & guests · stamfordyc.com

Walking

Shippan Point Shore Walk

A leisurely 3-mile loop around the peninsula past waterfront estates, marinas, and rocky coves. The best free activity in the neighborhood — especially at golden hour.

📍 Start at Shippan Ave · Open daily · Free

Tennis

Shippan Athletic Club

Private tennis and fitness club serving the Shippan community with indoor and outdoor courts, swimming, and a welcoming membership community for families.

📍 Shippan Point · Members & guests

Fishing

Sound Fishing

The rocky shoreline of Shippan Point offers some of the best shore fishing in Fairfield County. Striper season from May through November draws anglers from across the region.

📍 Shippan shoreline · CT fishing license required

Birdwatching

Shippan Point Birding

One of the best shorebird and waterfowl observation spots in Connecticut during migration seasons. Osprey, egrets, and herons nest along the shoreline throughout summer.

📍 Shippan Marshes · Dawn & dusk best · Free
