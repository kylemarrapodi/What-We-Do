Live Music & Bars

Where to Go

Live Music
Blue Light Live
📍 1806 Buddy Holly Ave

The soul of the Depot District. Intimate stage with the best Texas Red Dirt and singer-songwriter acts in the state. $5–$10 cover on most nights. Local legend: if you haven't been to Blue Light, you haven't been to Lubbock.

Show calendar →
Bar & Grill
Triple J Chophouse & Brew
📍 1807 Buddy Holly Ave

House-brewed craft beers, craft burgers, and a patio right in the heart of the Depot. A great first stop before heading to the music venues. The Triple J Burger is essential.

Live Music
Crickets Bar & Grill
📍 Depot District

Named for Buddy Holly's legendary band. Live music Thursday–Saturday with strong local and regional acts. Huge Texas craft beer selection and killer wings. Always packed after 9 PM.

Cocktail Bar
JoJo's Bohemia
📍 Depot District

Artsy, eclectic craft cocktail bar with mismatched furniture, local art on the walls, and a rotating drink menu. Live jazz and acoustic acts on weekends. The anti-frat bar in a great way.

Music Venue
Bash Riprock's
📍 Depot District

A Lubbock institution for decades. Rock and country cover bands, large dance floor, and cold beer. Gets absolutely packed on football Saturdays. The older crowd's pick in the Depot.

Patio Bar
The Depot District Patio Scene
📍 Around 19th St & Buddy Holly Ave

Multiple venues have outdoor patios connected to the street. On warm nights the whole block becomes one big outdoor party — music spills out of every door and people flow between venues freely.

Where to Eat

Depot District Dining

American · $$$
The Nicolett
📍 2407 19th St

The finest dining in the Depot District. House-aged steaks, seasonal menus, and an excellent Texas wine list. Book ahead for weekend game nights — it fills up fast.

Burgers · $
Triple J Chophouse
📍 1807 Buddy Holly Ave

Pre-show staple. House-ground burgers, fresh-cut fries, and the full craft beer menu. Loud and fun — not a quiet dinner spot, and that's the point.

Late Night · $
Food Trucks on 19th St
📍 19th St · Late nights Fri–Sat

After the shows wind down, food trucks line up on 19th Street. Tacos, loaded fries, elote, and late-night bites. The post-concert ritual for the Depot District crowd.
