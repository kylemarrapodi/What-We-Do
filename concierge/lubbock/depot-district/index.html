Live Music & Bars

Where to Go

Live Music
Blue Light Live
📍 1806 Buddy Holly Ave

The soul of the Depot District. Intimate stage with the best Texas Red Dirt and singer-songwriter acts in the state. Local legend: if you haven't been to Blue Light, you haven't been to Lubbock.

Show calendar →
Bar & Grill
Triple J Chophouse & Brew
📍 1807 Buddy Holly Ave

Lubbock's oldest brewpub, open since 2006 — house-brewed craft beers, steaks, and burgers right in the heart of the Depot. A great first stop before the music venues.

Outdoor Venue
The Garden
📍 1801 Buddy Holly Ave

String-lit outdoor bar and live music venue with weekend bands, yard games, rotating food trucks, and a pet-friendly patio.

Historic Theater
Cactus Theater
📍 1812 Buddy Holly Ave

Restored 1938 movie house directly across from Blue Light — live music, tribute shows, musicals, and a classic cinema series.

See shows →
College Bar
The Dean's List
📍 1701 Texas Ave

The Depot's newest bar, opened in the former Library Bar space on Texas Avenue with the same academic theme.

Winery
McPherson Cellars
📍 1615 Texas Ave

Texas High Plains wines poured in a restored 1930s Coca-Cola bottling plant — flights and bottles on a shady patio until 7 PM.

Where to Eat

Depot District Dining

Tapas · $$
La Diosa Cellars
📍 901 17th St

Spanish-inspired tapas and house wines from the only winery inside the Lubbock city limits — an eclectic, art-filled room a block off Buddy Holly Ave.

Steaks · Burgers · $$
Triple J Chophouse
📍 1807 Buddy Holly Ave

Pre-show staple. Steaks, house-ground burgers, and its own craft beer brewed on site. Loud and fun — not a quiet dinner spot, and that's the point.

Food Trucks · $
Food Trucks at The Garden
📍 1801 Buddy Holly Ave

The Garden hosts a rotating lineup of food trucks alongside its outdoor bar — the Depot District's go-to for a late bite between shows.
