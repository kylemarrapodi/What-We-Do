Where to Eat

Lubbock Dining

Texas BBQ, Tex-Mex, and a surprisingly deep restaurant scene. Here's where locals eat.

BBQ · $$
Evie Mae's Pit Barbecue
📍 217 US Hwy 62/82 · Wolfforth

The area's top-rated BBQ, just southwest of the city in Wolfforth — brisket and ribs that sell out daily.

Tex-Mex · $$
Abuelo's Mexican Restaurant
📍 Lubbock

Founded in Amarillo in 1989 and headquartered in Lubbock — a Tex-Mex standby for fajitas and margaritas.

Fine Dining · $$$
The Nicolett
📍 511 Broadway · Downtown

Chef Finn Walter's High Plains fine-dining destination — a Bon Appétit Best New Restaurants pick in 2022.

Steaks · Brewpub · $$
Triple J Chophouse & Brew
📍 1807 Buddy Holly Ave · Depot

Lubbock's oldest brewpub, pouring house-brewed beer with steaks and burgers in the Depot District since 2006.

Italian · $$
Orlando's Italian Restaurant
📍 2402 Ave Q · Near Campus

Serving family recipes since 1965 — Lubbock's oldest full-service restaurant, with a second location on Indiana Ave.

Breakfast · $
Cast Iron Grill
📍 620 19th St · Downtown

Scratch-made Southern breakfast and lunch, with a pie counter that stocks more than 30 homemade pies.

Burgers · $
Caprock Cafe
📍 3405 34th St · Central Lubbock

Char-broiled burgers, spicy appetizers, and cold beer — a neighborhood standby since 1997.

New American · $$$
The West Table Kitchen & Bar
📍 1204 Broadway · Downtown

Seasonal New American dining and craft cocktails in a restored downtown building, from the team behind The Brewery LBK.

Burgers · $
Blue Sky Texas
📍 3216 4th St · Near Campus

Handmade never-frozen burgers, fresh-cut fries, and shakes a few blocks from the Texas Tech campus.

Parks & Outdoors

Park / Trail
Mackenzie Park
📍 301 I-27 Frontage Rd · East Lubbock

900+ acres with Prairie Dog Town, jogging trails, and a miniature train — the largest park in Lubbock.

Nature
Lubbock Lake Landmark
📍 2401 Landmark Dr · North Lubbock

National Historic Landmark with 12,000 years of continuous human occupation history. Free.

Campus
Texas Tech University Campus

1,839-acre Spanish Renaissance campus, one of the most beautiful in the country.

Golf
