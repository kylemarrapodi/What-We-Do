Wreck 'Em Tech

Texas Tech Athletics

Red Raider games are the heartbeat of Lubbock. Every home game transforms the city — traffic, tailgates, bars packed from noon onwards.

Football · Big 12
Jones AT&T Stadium
📍 2526 6th St · Texas Tech Campus

60,454-seat home of Red Raider football. One of the most electric atmospheres in the Big 12. Tailgate scene on The Strip and Flint Ave is legendary — arrive early. Season: Sep–Nov.

Football schedule →
Basketball · Big 12
United Supermarkets Arena
📍 1701 Indiana Ave · Texas Tech Campus

15,098-seat arena hosting men's and women's basketball, plus major concerts and events. TTU basketball has been a Final Four contender in recent years. Season: Nov–Mar.

Arena events →
Baseball · Big 12
Dan Law Field / Rip Griffin Park
📍 Texas Tech Campus

Home of Red Raider baseball — one of the most consistent Big 12 programs. Intimate ballpark atmosphere with great sightlines. Season: Feb–May. National championship contender.

Baseball schedule →
Game Day
The Strip — University Ave
📍 University Ave · Near Campus

The main artery of game-day Lubbock. Bars like Whiskey Dicks, Depot, and the Varsity fill up hours before kickoff. Red and black everywhere. The official pre-game headquarters.

Women's Sports
TTU Women's Basketball
📍 United Supermarkets Arena

The Lady Raiders have been a Big 12 force for decades. Games at United Supermarkets Arena draw passionate crowds and strong rivalries with Baylor, UT, and Kansas.

Where to Eat

Lubbock Dining

Texas BBQ, Tex-Mex, and a surprisingly deep restaurant scene. Here's where locals eat.

BBQ · $$
Evie Mae's Pit Barbecue
📍 8108 Slide Rd · West Lubbock

The undisputed king of Lubbock BBQ. Brisket, ribs, sausage, and pulled pork that rival anything in Austin or the Hill Country. Sells out daily — arrive early.

Tex-Mex · $$
Abuelo's Mexican Restaurant
📍 Multiple locations · Lubbock

Lubbock-founded upscale Tex-Mex chain beloved citywide. Fajitas, margaritas, and enchiladas done right. The original location is where it all started.

American · $$$
The Nicolett
📍 2407 19th St · Depot District

Upscale American in the heart of the Depot District. Hand-cut steaks, locally-sourced ingredients, and one of the best wine lists in West Texas. Perfect for a game-night dinner.

Burgers · $
Triple J Chophouse & Brew
📍 1807 Buddy Holly Ave · Depot

Craft burgers and house-brewed beer steps from the music venues. A Depot District staple before a show. The Triple J Burger is iconic.

Italian · $$
Orlando's Italian Restaurant
📍 2402 Ave Q · Near Campus

Family-owned Italian institution loved by students and locals alike since the 1980s. Generous portions, BYOB wine policy, and pasta that'll have you coming back every visit.

Breakfast · $
J&L Home Cooking Restaurant
📍 3107 34th St · South Lubbock

The definitive Lubbock breakfast spot. Biscuits and gravy, chicken fried steak, and eggs the way West Texas does it. Cash only, lines out the door on weekends.

Arts & Culture

Venues & Institutions

Music Hall
Buddy Holly Hall of Performing Arts
📍 1300 Mac Davis Ln · Downtown

Opened 2021 — Lubbock's crown jewel. 2,200-seat Concert Hall and 450-seat Crickets Theatre. World-class acoustics hosting Broadway tours, symphonies, and major concerts.

See schedule →
Museum
Buddy Holly Center
📍 1801 Crickets Ave · Depot District

A must-visit. The definitive collection of Buddy Holly memorabilia, plus the West Texas Walk of Fame, and rotating exhibits on the Lubbock music scene.

Plan your visit →
Museum
National Ranching Heritage Center
📍 3121 4th St · Texas Tech Campus

37-acre outdoor museum with 48 authentic historic ranch structures. Tells the story of ranching from the 1780s to the 20th century. Free admission. A uniquely Texan experience.

Learn more →
Museum
Museum of Texas Tech University
📍 3301 4th St · Texas Tech Campus

One of the largest university museums in the US. Collections spanning natural science, anthropology, art, and history. The Moody Planetarium is a local favorite.

Visit info →
Concert Venue
United Supermarkets Arena
📍 1701 Indiana Ave · Texas Tech Campus

Beyond basketball, the USA hosts major national acts — recent shows include country legends, pop tours, and rodeo events. Check the calendar year-round.

Event calendar →
Winery
Cap*Rock Winery
📍 408 E Woodrow Rd · Lubbock

The Texas High Plains AVA produces excellent wine and Lubbock is at its center. Cap*Rock is the most accessible tasting room in the city — tours, tastings, and a stunning view.

After Dark

Nightlife & Live Music

Lubbock has a surprisingly rich live music culture. The Depot District and The Strip are where the action is every weekend.

Live Music
Blue Light Live
📍 1806 Buddy Holly Ave · Depot District

The quintessential Lubbock music venue. Texas singer-songwriters, outlaw country, and Red Dirt music every weekend. Intimate, warm, and completely authentic.

See shows →
Country Bar
Whiskey Dicks
📍 2419 University Ave · The Strip

The Strip's most legendary bar. Country music, mechanical bull, cheap beer, and wall-to-wall Red Raiders gear. The pre-game bar for home games. Opens early on football Saturdays.

Dive Bar
The Library Bar
📍 1516 University Ave · The Strip

A Texas Tech institution. Pool tables, cold beer, and a relaxed crowd. The kind of bar you tell people you're going to "the library." Live music some weekends.

Live Music
Crickets Bar & Grill
📍 Depot District

Named for Buddy Holly's band, Crickets is a cornerstone of the Depot District scene. Live music Thursday–Saturday, strong Texas craft beer selection, great wings.

Cocktail Bar
JoJo's Bohemia
📍 Depot District

Craft cocktails and an eclectic, artsy vibe in the Depot District. Popular with the TTU arts crowd. Live jazz and acoustic on weekends. Great for a date or a night out with friends.

Rooftop Bar
1520 Bar & Lounge
📍 1520 University Ave · The Strip

Multi-level bar with rooftop views of the campus and city. DJ nights on weekends, craft cocktails, and a VIP section that's always hopping after TTU home games.

Get Outside

Parks & Outdoors

Park / Trail
Mackenzie Park
📍 301 I-27 Frontage Rd · East Lubbock

900+ acres with a golf course, Prairie Dog Town, jogging trails, a miniature train, and the Meadowbrook Golf Course. The largest public park in Lubbock and a local landmark.

Nature
Lubbock Lake Landmark
📍 2401 Landmark Dr · North Lubbock

National Historic Landmark with 12,000 years of continuous human occupation history. Walking trails through the playa lake ecosystem. Archaeology museum on-site. Free.

Campus
Texas Tech University Campus
📍 Texas Tech Pkwy · West Lubbock

1,839-acre Spanish Renaissance campus is one of the most beautiful in the country. Walk the Memorial Circle, Carol of Lights at Christmas, and the stunning architecture.

Golf
Rawls Course at Texas Tech
📍 3720 4th St · Texas Tech Campus

One of the top public university golf courses in the country. Designed by Tom Doak, it has hosted NCAA Regionals. Stunning prairie landscape design. Open to the public.

Tee times →
Lake
Buffalo Springs Lake
📍 9999 High Meadow Rd · Outside Lubbock

The closest real swimming, camping, and fishing to Lubbock. Water sports, kayaking, and a beach area. Popular weekend destination for locals. 15 minutes from downtown.

Wineries
Texas High Plains Wine Trail
📍 Multiple locations · Lubbock area

The High Plains AVA produces 80% of Texas's wine grapes. A half-dozen tasting rooms within 30 minutes of downtown — Cap*Rock, McPherson Cellars, and Llano Estacado are the anchors.
