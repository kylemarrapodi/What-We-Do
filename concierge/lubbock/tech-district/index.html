The Strip

University Ave Bars & Hangouts

Country Bar
Whiskey Dicks
📍 2419 University Ave

The Strip's crown jewel. Mechanical bull, country music, cheap domestics, and a sea of red and black jerseys on game days. Opens at noon on football Saturdays. The energy is unmatched.

Dive Bar
The Library Bar
📍 1516 University Ave

A Texas Tech institution for decades. Pool tables, cold beer, unpretentious atmosphere. "I'm going to the library" has meant this bar for generations of Red Raiders.

Rooftop Bar
1520 Bar & Lounge
📍 1520 University Ave

Multi-level with rooftop views of campus. DJ nights Friday–Saturday, craft cocktails, and a VIP section. The upscale option on The Strip — but still packed with Red Raiders gear.

Sports Bar
The Varsity
📍 University Ave

Every TV tuned to Red Raider games. The sports bar go-to for every away game and the overflow crowd for home games. Full food menu, wings, and a massive selection of draft beers.

Patio Bar
Lonesome Dove Western Bistro
📍 Near Campus

Upscale Western cuisine and cocktails with a patio. One of the top pre-game dinner spots near campus. Reservations recommended on home game weekends.

Pizza · $
Manny's Pit BBQ & More
📍 Near Texas Tech Campus

Late-night campus staple. BBQ sandwiches, burritos, and loaded nachos. The go-to fuel stop between The Strip and heading home. Open late on weekends.

Campus Life

Texas Tech University

Campus
Jones AT&T Stadium
📍 2526 6th St

Home of Red Raider football since 1947. 60,454 seats, a Double-T painted at midfield, and one of the loudest atmospheres in the Big 12. Tour available on non-game days.

Schedule →
Campus
United Supermarkets Arena
📍 1701 Indiana Ave

Home of Red Raider basketball plus major touring concerts and events. 15,098 seats. The USA is the main events hub of Lubbock.

Event calendar →
Golf
Rawls Course at Texas Tech
📍 3720 4th St

Tom Doak-designed course consistently ranked among the top public university courses in the US. Has hosted NCAA Regional play. Stunning South Plains landscape. Open to the public.

Tee times →
Walking
Memorial Circle
📍 Texas Tech Campus · Center

The iconic center of campus. Spanish Renaissance architecture, massive oak trees, and the Saddle Tramp statue. Walk the circle — it's what every TTU student and visitor does on their first visit.

Museum
National Ranching Heritage Center
📍 3121 4th St

37-acre outdoor museum with 48 authentic historic ranch structures from the 1780s–1950s. Completely free admission. One of the most underrated museums in Texas.

Plan your visit →
Event
Carol of Lights — December
📍 Texas Tech Campus · Early December

One of the most beautiful holiday traditions in Texas. 25,000+ lights cover the administration buildings while the TTU choir performs. Free and open to the public. Draws tens of thousands.

Where to Eat

Near Campus Dining

Italian · $$
Orlando's Italian Restaurant
📍 2402 Ave Q

A Texas Tech institution since the 1980s. BYOB wine policy, massive portions, and authentic family-style Italian. The pasta is some of the best in West Texas. Always packed.

Wings · $
Wings-N-More
📍 Near Campus

The game-watch wings spot. 40+ flavors, giant beer towers, and every TV on Red Raider games. The campus crowd's go-to for away games and late-night post-Strip food.

Diner · $
Spanky's Restaurant & Bar
📍 1811 Buddy Holly Ave

Campus diner classic with comfort food and famously large portions. A lunch destination for professors, students, and anyone who discovers it. The chicken fried steak is mandatory.
