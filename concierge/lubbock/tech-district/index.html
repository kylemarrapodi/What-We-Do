The Strip

University Ave Bars & Hangouts

Draft House
Cricket's Draft House + Grill
📍 2412 Broadway

Named for Buddy Holly's band and a Broadway fixture since 1996 — a huge draft selection, from-scratch food, and a full gaming area. Packed on game days.

Sports Bar
Bash Riprock's
📍 2419 Main St

A longtime Lubbock sports bar a block off Broadway near campus — cold beer and every TV on the Red Raiders.

Tex-Mex Bar
Chimy's Cerveceria
📍 2417 Broadway

Chimichangas, cheap margaritas, and a rowdy patio steps from campus — the definitive Tech pre-game and post-game spot.

Campus Life

Texas Tech University

Campus
Jones AT&T Stadium
📍 2526 6th St

Home of Red Raider football since 1947. 60,454 seats, a Double-T painted at midfield, and one of the loudest atmospheres in the Big 12. Tour available on non-game days.

Schedule →
Campus
United Supermarkets Arena
📍 1701 Indiana Ave

Home of Red Raider basketball plus major touring concerts and events. 15,098 seats. The USA is the main events hub of Lubbock.

Event calendar →
Golf
Rawls Course at Texas Tech
📍 3720 4th St

Tom Doak-designed course consistently ranked among the top public university courses in the US. Has hosted NCAA Regional play. Stunning South Plains landscape. Open to the public.

Tee times →
Walking
Memorial Circle
📍 Texas Tech Campus · Center

The iconic center of campus. Spanish Renaissance architecture, massive oak trees, and the Saddle Tramp statue. Walk the circle — it's what every TTU student and visitor does on their first visit.

Museum
National Ranching Heritage Center
📍 3121 4th St

37-acre outdoor museum with 48 authentic historic ranch structures from the 1780s–1950s. Completely free admission. One of the most underrated museums in Texas.

Plan your visit →
Event
Carol of Lights — Dec 6, 2026
📍 Science Quadrangle · Texas Tech Campus

One of the most beautiful holiday traditions in Texas — the 68th annual ceremony is Sunday, Dec 6, 2026: carillon concert 6:30 PM, ceremony 7 PM, 30,000+ lights across campus. Free and open to the public.

Where to Eat

Near Campus Dining

Italian · $$
Orlando's Italian Restaurant
📍 2402 Ave Q

Serving family recipes since 1965 — Lubbock's oldest full-service restaurant, run by Texas Tech grads. Massive portions and the famous green chile chicken linguine. Always packed.

Burgers · $
Blue Sky Texas
📍 3216 4th St

Handmade never-frozen burgers, fresh-cut fries, onion rings, and shakes a few blocks from campus — with a Tech student discount.

Diner · $
Spanky's Restaurant & Bar
📍 811 University Ave

Across from campus at 19th and University since 1982 — old-style burgers, cold beer, and the famous fried cheese. A rite of passage for every Red Raider.
