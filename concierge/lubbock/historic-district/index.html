Arts & Culture

Venues & Museums

Concert Hall
Buddy Holly Hall of Performing Arts
📍 1300 Mac Davis Ln

Opened 2021 — the most significant cultural venue built in Lubbock in a generation. 2,200-seat Concert Hall with superb acoustics plus a 450-seat Crickets Theatre. Broadway tours, symphonies, and major national acts.

Full schedule →
Museum
Buddy Holly Center
📍 1801 Crickets Ave · Depot District

The definitive collection on Buddy Holly and the rock & roll era. Original memorabilia, instruments, and a fine arts gallery. The West Texas Walk of Fame is outside. Unmissable for music lovers.

Plan your visit →
Landmark
Buddy Holly Statue & Walk of Fame
📍 8th St & Ave Q · Downtown

Life-size bronze statue of Buddy Holly in his signature pose stands at the corner of 8th and Q. The West Texas Walk of Fame honors Lubbock music legends including Waylon Jennings and Mac Davis.

Symphony
Lubbock Symphony Orchestra
📍 Buddy Holly Hall · Downtown

The LSO performs at Buddy Holly Hall year-round — classical concerts, pops series, and holiday performances. A subscription season is one of the best value entertainment options in Lubbock.

Season schedule →
Theatre
Lubbock Community Theatre
📍 Downtown Lubbock

One of the oldest community theatres in Texas. Musicals, dramas, and comedies throughout the season. Affordable tickets and consistently high-quality local productions.

Winery
McPherson Cellars
📍 1615 Texas Ave · Downtown

Award-winning Texas winery in a beautifully restored 1930s Coca-Cola bottling plant downtown. The best indoor tasting room in the city — also hosts concerts and private events.

Where to Eat

Downtown & Historic District Dining

American · $$$
The Nicolett
📍 2407 19th St

Lubbock's upscale dining anchor. Hand-cut steaks, seasonal menus with West Texas ingredients, and an impressive wine list anchored by Texas High Plains bottles.

Tex-Mex · $$
Rosa's Café & Tortilla Factory
📍 Multiple locations · Downtown

West Texas Tex-Mex done right. Fresh tortillas made on-site, massive burritos, and queso that'll ruin you for all other queso. A Lubbock institution born and raised in the city.

Steakhouse · $$$
Cagle Steaks
📍 Downtown Lubbock

Old-school West Texas steakhouse with beef you can trust. Lubbock sits at the heart of the Texas cattle industry — the steaks here prove it. A local institution for special occasions.
