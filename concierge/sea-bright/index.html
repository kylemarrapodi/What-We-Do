Sea Bright

A three-mile ribbon of barrier peninsula wedged between the Atlantic Ocean and the Shrewsbury River — often called the first beach town at the Jersey Shore heading south from Sandy Hook. Sea Bright runs on a dense little strip of Ocean Avenue restaurants, membership beach clubs tucked behind a 19th-century seawall, and a fishing and surfing culture packed into just 0.72 square miles of land. The whole town rebuilt itself almost from scratch after Hurricane Sandy came ashore in October 2012.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Monmouth County, on the barrier peninsula between the Shrewsbury River and the Atlantic.

🏖

Beach badges: Required Memorial Day–Labor Day at supervised public beaches. $8/day, free under 12.

🚗

Parking: Metered downtown lot, 9am–9pm (May 15–Sep 15): $1/hr weekdays, $2/hr weekends. The small Anchorage Beach lot fills fast.

🌉

Getting there: Route 36 over the Highlands–Sea Bright Bridge from the north, or the Rumson–Sea Bright Bridge from the west.

🏊

Beach clubs: Several membership-only clubs line Ocean Ave — Driftwood, Sands, Ship Ahoy, Surfrider and others. Non-members need a badge for the public sections.

📅

Peak season: Memorial Day–Labor Day. Many restaurants and clubs scale back or close in the off-season.

Quick Tips

⏰

The downtown lot fills by late morning on summer weekends — arrive early or plan to walk from Rumson.

🎣

Shrewsbury Riverfront Park is the only spot to fish without a club membership or a boat.

🌅

River-side restaurants like Eventide Grille and Drifthouse face west — book early for a sunset table.

🍹

Donovan's Reef is walk-up and cash-friendly for a beach drink; the beach clubs are members-only.
