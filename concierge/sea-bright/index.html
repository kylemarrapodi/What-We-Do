Sea Bright

A three-mile ribbon of barrier peninsula wedged between the Atlantic Ocean and the Shrewsbury River — often called the first beach town at the Jersey Shore heading south from Sandy Hook. Sea Bright runs on a dense little strip of Ocean Avenue restaurants, membership beach clubs tucked behind a 19th-century seawall, and a fishing and surfing culture packed into just 0.72 square miles of land. The whole town rebuilt itself almost from scratch after Hurricane Sandy came ashore in October 2012.

Attractions

Public BeachSurfing

Sea Bright Municipal Beach

The town's guarded public beach, with a jetty and surf breaks outside the swimming area. Daily beach badges are required Memorial Day through Labor Day ($8/day, free under 12); non-members also need a badge to use the sections in front of the private clubs. A newly renovated pavilion anchors the main downtown lot.

📍 Ocean Ave · Badges required in season · Guarded

Fishing & Kayaking

Shrewsbury Riverfront Park

The only public fishing spot in Sea Bright — a fishing pier and cleaning station on the Shrewsbury River, just across the Rumson–Sea Bright Bridge. Also has a public kayak launch on Front Street, 810 feet of riverfront boardwalk, a small dog park, and calmer water than the ocean side for paddling.

📍 Front St, riverside · Free · Kayak launch & fishing pier

Since 1957Beach Club

Driftwood Cabana Club

A family-run beach club on the ocean since 1957, with beachfront cabanas, an Olympic-size pool, a spray park, tennis, a tiki bar, and the Drifthouse restaurant on site. One of several membership beach clubs that line Ocean Avenue and give Sea Bright its country-club-on-the-sand character.

📍 1485 Ocean Ave · Membership club · Seasonal

History

Sea Bright–Monmouth Beach Seawall

A 4.7-mile seawall running along the barrier peninsula, first built more than a century ago to protect the rail line (now Route 36) from Atlantic storms. It's the reason Sea Bright exists where it does — and it took a direct hit from Hurricane Sandy's storm surge in 2012 before the town rebuilt around and behind it.

📍 Runs the length of Ocean Ave · Route 36

Dining & Nightlife

ItalianSince 1996

Anjelica's Restaurant

One of the Jersey Shore's better-known Italian dining rooms, family-owned and named for the owner's daughter. The menu leans on recipes with roots in Avellino, Sicily and Lombardy alongside familiar Italian-American plates. Rebuilt from the ground up after Hurricane Sandy and reopened about a year later.

📍 1070 Ocean Ave · Reservations recommended

Sports BarTop Rated

Tommy's Tavern + Tap

A rustic-industrial tavern with three specialty kitchens — coal-fired pizza, tavern fare and sushi — plus 24 rotating draft taps and 34 TVs. Riverside cabanas and covered porch seating look out over the Shrewsbury. Repeatedly voted Monmouth County's #1 sports bar and restaurant.

📍 1030 Ocean Ave · (732) 842-5044

Seafood · SushiAward-Winning

Drifthouse

Oceanfront restaurant, lounge and bar at the Driftwood Cabana Club, serving sushi and seafood, dry-aged steaks, a raw bar and house-made pasta in a beach-chic room built for sunset views over the Shrewsbury. Named "Best New Restaurant" by both NJ Monthly and the Star-Ledger.

📍 1485 Ocean Ave · Waterfront · Cocktails

American

Eventide Grille at Navesink Marina

A true neighborhood restaurant on the Shrewsbury River, known for its sunsets as much as its menu of sandwiches, tacos and seafood. Casual, marina-side, and a favorite for boaters coming in off the water.

📍 1400 Ocean Ave, Navesink Marina · (732) 530-1414

Oceanfront BarSince 1976

Donovan's Reef

One of the only bars in New Jersey that serves cocktails on the sand — a private, lifeguarded beach bar named after the John Wayne film, open since 1976. A rogue wave during Hurricane Sandy shoved the building 30 feet off its foundation; it was rebuilt and reopened. Live music and DJs run Friday through Sunday in season.

📍 1171 Ocean Ave · Beach bar · Live music weekends

Tap & Grill

Ocean House Tap and Grill

A casual tap-and-grill spot on Ocean Avenue with a rotating beer list and a straightforward pub menu — burgers, apps and bar food done well, a block from the beach.

📍 1072 Ocean Ave · (732) 704-3620

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Monmouth County, on the barrier peninsula between the Shrewsbury River and the Atlantic.

🏖

Beach badges: Required Memorial Day–Labor Day at supervised public beaches. $8/day, free under 12.

🚗

Parking: Metered downtown lot, 9am–9pm (May 15–Sep 15): $1/hr weekdays, $2/hr weekends. The small Anchorage Beach lot fills fast.

🌉

Getting there: Route 36 over the Highlands–Sea Bright Bridge from the north, or the Rumson–Sea Bright Bridge from the west.

🏊

Beach clubs: Several membership-only clubs line Ocean Ave — Driftwood, Sands, Ship Ahoy, Surfrider and others. Non-members need a badge for the public sections.

📅

Peak season: Memorial Day–Labor Day. Many restaurants and clubs scale back or close in the off-season.

Quick Tips

⏰

The downtown lot fills by late morning on summer weekends — arrive early or plan to walk from Rumson.

🎣

Shrewsbury Riverfront Park is the only spot to fish without a club membership or a boat.

🌅

River-side restaurants like Eventide Grille and Drifthouse face west — book early for a sunset table.

🍹

Donovan's Reef is walk-up and cash-friendly for a beach drink; the beach clubs are members-only.
