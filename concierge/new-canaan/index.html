New Canaan

A small town with an outsized design legacy. Established as Canaan Parish in 1731, New Canaan became the proving ground of American modernism after World War II, when the Harvard Five — Philip Johnson among them — built roughly 80 experimental homes in its woods, including Johnson's own Glass House. Today it runs on a compact, walkable village center around Elm and Main, the 300-acre spread of Waveny Park, and its own Metro-North branch line — the source of the town's century-old nickname.

Attractions & Outdoors

Modern ArchitectureNational Trust Site

The Glass House

Philip Johnson's 1949 masterpiece and its 49-acre campus of structures. The 2026 tour season runs April 16 – December 14, closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays; tours start at the Visitor Center + Design Store at 199 Elm St, and advance tickets are strongly recommended — they sell out.

📍 Visitor Center: 199 Elm St · theglasshouse.org
Architecture & Nature80 Acres

Grace Farms

The SANAA-designed River building — a ribbon of glass volumes under one winding roof — set in 80 acres of meadow and woodland, with an amphitheater, tea room and library. Open Tue–Sat 10 AM–5 PM, Sun 12–5 PM, closed Mondays.

📍 365 Lukes Wood Rd · gracefarms.org
Town Park300 Acres

Waveny Park

The town's great lawn — 300 acres of fields, ponds and 3.5 miles of woodland trails around the 1912 Waveny House ("the castle"). Open daylight hours, seven days a week, and home of the fall Caffeine & Carburetors.

📍 677 South Ave · Free
Nature Center

New Canaan Nature Center

Forty acres of woods, meadows and ponds with two miles of trails just north of the village; the visitor buildings are open Tue–Sat 9 AM–4 PM.

📍 144 Oenoke Ridge

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Inland Fairfield County — Stamford to the south and west, Darien to the south, Wilton to the east, and the New York line to the north.

🚆

Getting there: Metro-North's New Canaan Branch ends here — two stops in town (Talmadge Hill and New Canaan), about 65 minutes to Grand Central with the branch connection.

🚗

Driving: The Merritt Parkway crosses the south of town; the village center sits a few minutes north of it.

🏷

The nickname: "The Next Station to Heaven" — an 1890s newspaper editor's comeback after a rival called the branch line's terminus the next station to the other place.

🏗

Design pilgrimage: About 80 mid-century modern homes were built here by the Harvard Five and their circle; roughly 20 have been lost, and the Glass House is the one you can actually tour.

Quick Tips

🎟

Glass House tours sell out — book online before you come; there's no walk-up guarantee at 199 Elm St.

☕

For Caffeine & Carburetors, arrive close to 8 AM — the rarest metal tends to leave before 11.

📅

Grace Farms and the Nature Center are both closed Mondays; Rosie is too. Plan a Tuesday–Saturday visit to catch everything.

🥾

Waveny's trails are free and open daylight hours — park at the South Avenue entrance.
