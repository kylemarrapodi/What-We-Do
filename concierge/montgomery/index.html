Montgomery Township

A 32-square-mile township of old Dutch farmland and new-money cul-de-sacs a few miles north of Princeton, with no single downtown — instead three named villages, Skillman, Belle Mead and Blawenburg, scattered across rolling fields, historic churches and a Route 206 corridor that does most of the everyday business. Montgomery is regularly ranked among New Jersey's wealthiest townships, and still looks, in patches, like the 18th-century farm country it grew out of.

Need to Know

🗺

Location: Somerset County, bordering Princeton to the south and Hillsborough, Hopewell and Franklin townships.

🏘

Villages: Skillman, Belle Mead and Blawenburg are unincorporated communities within the township, not separate towns — along with smaller Harlingen.

🚗

Getting there: Route 206 runs the length of the township north–south; County Routes 518 and 601 (Great Road) cross it and reach Blawenburg and Skillman.

🏪

No real downtown: Most everyday business — shops, restaurants, services — clusters along the Route 206 corridor rather than in the historic village centers.

💰

Character: Regularly ranked among New Jersey's wealthiest municipalities, with a median household income above $180,000.

Quick Tips

🚦

Blawenburg's historic crossroads is easy to miss at highway speed on CR 518 — worth the deliberate stop.

🚲

Skillman Park's paved 2.25-mile loop is the easiest trail in the township for strollers and bikes.

🍽

Nearly everything to eat is on Route 206, not inside the historic villages — plan around the corridor.

✈️

Princeton Airport (despite the name) is a Montgomery Township landmark, not a Princeton one.
